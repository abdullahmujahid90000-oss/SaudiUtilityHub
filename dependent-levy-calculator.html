👨‍👩‍👧 Dependent Fee Calculator Saudi Arabia 2026

Estimate the monthly fee for a sponsored spouse or child under 18. Not for domestic workers — see why below.

SAR 400/month per dependent · in effect since July 2020[1]
The Saudi dependent levy (companion fee) is SAR 400 per month for each non-Saudi spouse or child under 18 sponsored on your iqama, and has been at that level since July 1, 2020. It does not include domestic workers, who are handled under a separate system — this estimator only covers family dependents.
Who this estimator covers: a sponsored spouse and/or children under 18 (the "companions"/"dependents" category under Ministry of Interior rules).
Who it excludes: domestic workers (drivers, housemaids, etc. — recruited via Musaned/HRSD, a different system with different fees we have not verified here), children 18 and over (who cannot remain as standard dependents), and parents (sponsoring parents follows separate, restrictive rules not covered by this page).

📖 How to Use This Estimator

This estimator computes the SAR 400/month Saudi dependent levy for a sponsored spouse and/or children under 18. It does not include domestic workers, and it does not connect to Absher, the General Directorate of Passports, or any government record — it only performs the arithmetic below on the numbers you enter.

  1. Enter your sponsored family dependents
    Select whether a spouse is sponsored, and enter the number of children under 18 who are sponsored on your iqama.
  2. Enter the payment period
    Enter how many months you want to estimate for (1–24). The fee is charged in whole-month units in advance, so round up to the next full month for any partial month.
  3. Review the breakdown
    See the fee broken down by spouse and children, the monthly subtotal, and the total for your chosen period.
  4. Confirm the exact amount before paying
    Use Absher to confirm your exact amount due before making any payment.

Renewal periods and partial months

The dependent levy is collected in advance — typically when your iqama is issued or renewed, or when an exit/re-entry or final exit visa is issued[1]. It is charged per whole month, not prorated by day. If a dependent is sponsored partway through a month, we assume that month counts in full, matching how the fee is understood to be collected. This estimator does not know your specific renewal date — use it to estimate a period you specify, not as a lookup of your actual due date.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Saudi dependent levy and how much is it?
The dependent levy (also called the companion fee) is a fee charged for each non-Saudi spouse or child under 18 sponsored on an expatriate's iqama. It has been SAR 400 per dependent per month since July 1, 2020, after being phased in at SAR 100/month (2017), SAR 200/month (2018), and SAR 300/month (2019). Source: Saudi Press Agency reporting on the General Directorate of Passports announcement.
Does the dependent levy apply to domestic workers?
No. This estimator does not include domestic workers (such as drivers or housemaids), because the SAR 400/month dependent levy applies specifically to companions of expatriate workers, not to domestic labor visas. Domestic workers are recruited and residency-processed through a separate system (the Musaned platform, run by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development), with its own visa and residency fees. We have not found a current primary government source confirming a monthly per-worker fee for domestic workers comparable to the dependent levy, so we do not estimate one — confirm any domestic-worker fees directly through Musaned or Absher.
When and how is the dependent levy collected?
It is collected in advance, typically when an iqama is issued or renewed, or when an exit/re-entry or final exit visa is issued for the sponsor. You can pay for multiple months at once. Source: Saudi Press Agency / General Directorate of Passports.
Does this tool check my exact amount due with the government?
No. This is an independent estimator, not an official checker. It does not connect to Absher, the General Directorate of Passports, or any government system. Always confirm your exact amount due through Absher before paying.
Are there exemptions from the dependent levy?
Some categories, such as dependents of GCC nationals or diplomatic staff, are commonly reported as exempt or treated differently, but we have not independently verified the current, complete exemption list against a primary government source. If you believe you may be exempt, confirm your specific case through Absher rather than relying on this page.
Is the dependent levy the same as the iqama renewal fee?
No, they are different fees charged on different schedules. The iqama renewal fee is a separate cost (see our Iqama Expiry Estimator); the dependent levy is the SAR 400/month-per-dependent fee estimated on this page. Both can be due around the same renewal event.
