How Much Is the Dependent Fee in 2026?

The dependent fee (also called the dependent levy, family fee or "maqabil maliy lil murafiqeen") is SAR 400 per dependent per month, which is SAR 4,800 a year for each person. It started at SAR 100 in 2017, went up by SAR 100 every July, and has stayed at SAR 400 since July 2020.

This is separate from the SAR 800 monthly expat worker levy, which your employer pays for you. The dependent fee is the sponsor's own cost, and in most cases the employer does not cover it unless your contract says so.

Who Pays and Who Is Exempt

  • Pays: any expat iqama holder sponsoring a wife, husband, children or parents on a family residence visa.
  • Exempt: Saudi citizens, and Premium Residency holders and their families.
  • Not part of this fee: domestic workers such as drivers and housemaids. They are handled through Musaned and have their own costs.

A newborn is counted once the baby is added to your iqama, so expect the fee to rise at your next renewal.

When and How You Pay

The fee is collected in advance, for the whole period, at the moment you renew a dependent's iqama, issue an exit re-entry visa, or process a final exit. That is why the SADAD amount at renewal often comes as a shock: a family of four on a 12-month renewal is SAR 14,400 in one go.

  1. Check the amount due in Absher using the "Query about the Financial Consideration for Companions" service.
  2. In your banking app, open SADAD → Government Payments → Ministry of Interior (Passports) → Dependents Fee, and enter your iqama number.
  3. Pay the amount shown. It usually reflects in Absher within minutes.
  4. Go back to Absher and complete the renewal or visa.

What Happens If You Do Not Pay

  • Your family's iqamas cannot be renewed.
  • No exit re-entry or final exit visa can be issued for the family until it is cleared.
  • If an iqama expires because the fee was not paid, late-renewal fines start at SAR 500 for the first offence and double for a second.

If the full year is too much at once, renewing for a shorter period where Absher allows it reduces the amount you pay upfront. You pay the same per month either way.

Worked Example

Ahmed works in Riyadh and sponsors his wife and two children. His iqama renewal comes up in March and he renews for 12 months.

3 dependents × SAR 400 × 12 months = SAR 14,400. If his wife arrives mid-year instead, with 5 months left before his next renewal, her fee for that first period is 5 × SAR 400 = SAR 2,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the dependent fee still SAR 400 per month in 2026?
Yes. It has been SAR 400 per dependent per month since July 2020, after rising from SAR 100 in 2017.
Do I pay the dependent fee for a newborn baby?
Yes, once the baby has an iqama under your sponsorship. The fee is added from that point.
Does my employer pay the dependent fee?
Normally no. The fee for your family is your responsibility as the sponsor. A few employers cover it as a benefit, so check your contract.
Can I pay the dependent fee monthly?
Not month by month. It is collected in advance for the period you renew or travel. Choosing a shorter renewal period, where Absher offers it, lowers the upfront amount.
Is the fee refunded if my family leaves on final exit?
The fee is linked to the iqama period you paid for. If your family leaves early, ask Jawazat through Absher about any unused months, but do not count on a refund.
Is the dependent fee the same as the iqama renewal fee?
No. The iqama renewal fee is a separate charge. Both are usually paid at the same time, which is why renewal day is expensive.
