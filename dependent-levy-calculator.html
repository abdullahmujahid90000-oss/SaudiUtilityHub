Dependent Fee Calculator: Know Your Renewal Bill Before It Arrives

SAR 400 per family member, per month, paid upfront. Here's exactly what your family costs, and how to make renewal day painless.

SAR 400 per dependentAbsher & SADAD stepsUpdated November 2026
Home › Dependent Fee Calculator

Every expat family knows the feeling. Iqama renewal day arrives, you open SADAD, and the number on the screen is far bigger than you remembered. Most of that number is usually the dependent fee, and it's easy to plan for once you see how it works.

Short answer: the dependent fee is SAR 400 per family member per month, which is SAR 4,800 a year each. It's paid in advance for the whole renewal period, by you as the sponsor, not your employer. A wife and two children on a 12-month renewal comes to SAR 14,400 in one payment.

The fee started at SAR 100 in 2017, rose by SAR 100 every July, and has stayed at SAR 400 since July 2020. It's often called the dependent levy, family fee or maqabil maliy lil murafiqeen. They're all the same charge.

Ahmed's Renewal: A Real-World Example

Ahmed is an accountant in Riyadh earning SAR 9,500. He sponsors his wife and two children, and his renewal comes up in March. He renews everyone for 12 months:

3 dependents × SAR 400 × 12 months = SAR 14,400

That's about a month and a half of his salary, due on one day. Ahmed now sets aside SAR 1,200 every month in a separate account, so renewal day costs him nothing extra.

When his wife's mother came to live with them on a family visa, she was added to his iqama too. That's another SAR 400 a month from her first renewal.

Who Pays, and Who Doesn't

  • You pay if you're an expat iqama holder sponsoring a wife, husband, children or parents.
  • Your employer pays the separate SAR 800 monthly worker levy for you, but not the fee for your family. That's yours unless your contract says otherwise.
  • Exempt: Saudi citizens, and Premium Residency holders and their families.
  • Not included: housemaids and drivers. Domestic workers go through Musaned and have their own costs.

A newborn counts once the baby has an iqama under you, so expect the next renewal to go up by SAR 400 a month.

How to Pay It in Five Minutes

  1. In Absher, check the amount due with the "Query about the Financial Consideration for Companions" service.
  2. In your banking app: SADAD → Government Payments → Ministry of Interior (Passports) → Dependents Fee, then enter your iqama number.
  3. Pay the amount shown. It normally appears in Absher within minutes.
  4. Go back to Absher and finish the renewal, or issue the exit re-entry or final exit.
The fee is also collected when you issue an exit re-entry or final exit for a dependant, not only at renewal. Check Absher before booking flights.

Mistakes That Cost Families Money

  • Waiting for renewal week. If the money isn't there, the iqama expires, and late-renewal fines start at SAR 500 for the first offence and double the second time.
  • Forgetting that dependants have their own expiry dates. Your wife's and children's iqamas may not expire on the same day as yours. Check each one.
  • Booking a holiday before paying. No dependant exit re-entry can be issued while the fee is outstanding.
  • Not using a shorter renewal. If a full year is too much at once, renew for a shorter period where Absher allows it. The monthly cost is the same; you just pay less upfront.
  • Comparing job offers on gross salary alone. A SAR 8,000 offer with a family of four on your iqama is really closer to SAR 6,800 once the fee is taken out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the dependent fee still SAR 400 per month?
Yes. It has been SAR 400 per dependent per month since July 2020, after rising from SAR 100 in 2017.
Do I pay the dependent fee for a newborn baby?
Yes, once the baby has an iqama under your sponsorship. The fee is added from then, so your next renewal will be SAR 400 a month higher.
Does my employer pay the dependent fee?
Normally no. Your employer pays the SAR 800 worker levy for you, but the fee for your family is yours unless your contract says the company covers it.
Can I pay the dependent fee monthly?
No. It's collected in advance for the period you renew or travel. A shorter renewal period, where Absher offers it, lowers the upfront amount.
Is the fee refunded if my family leaves on final exit?
Don't count on it. The fee is tied to the period you paid for. You can ask Jawazat through Absher about unused months.
How much is the dependent fee for a family of four?
For a wife and two children (three dependents), it's SAR 1,200 a month, or SAR 14,400 for a 12-month renewal.
