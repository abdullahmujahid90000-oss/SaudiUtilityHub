🛂 Iqama Renewal Blocked by an Expired Passport? Fix It in This Order

Absher rejected your iqama renewal and the reason is your passport. Here's the order to fix it, who has to press which button, and a timeline that keeps you clear of fines.

🛂 Passport first🔄 Absher & Muqeem update⏱️ Avoid late fines
Home › Guides › Iqama Renewal With Expired Passport

You open Absher to renew your iqama and get the error you were dreading: the passport is expired, or about to be. The renewal won't go through, the expiry date is getting closer, and your embassy's next appointment is weeks away. Don't panic. There's a clear order to fix this, and once you know who presses which button, it's very manageable.

Short answer: you can't renew an iqama on an expired passport, or one about to expire. Renew the passport first at your embassy or consulate in Saudi Arabia. Then the new passport must be updated on your iqama record: your employer does this for you through Absher Business or Muqeem, and you do it yourself for family members in Absher. Once the record shows the new passport, the iqama renews as normal.

Step by Step

  1. Renew your passport at your embassy or consulate. Book early, because some take several weeks.
  2. Pay any traffic fines. The passport update is blocked while there are unpaid violations on the iqama.
  3. Update the passport details. For yourself, ask HR to use "update passport information" in Absher Business or Muqeem, because workers can't update their own passport. For dependants, go to your Absher account, then Family Members, then Update Passport Information.
  4. Check in Absher that the new passport number now shows on the iqama.
  5. Renew the iqama after paying the iqama fee, the work permit levy and any dependent fees through SADAD.

Conditions to Know

  • The person must be inside Saudi Arabia when the update is done.
  • The online update is limited in how often it can be used and in how early before the old passport's expiry you can use it. If Absher refuses, the message usually says which condition failed.
  • If the old passport was lost, not just expired, report the loss first. Updating a lost passport goes through Jawazat and carries a fine.
  • A small service fee applies to the online passport update.

Real Example: Sanjay's Family Renewal

Sanjay's iqama, and his wife's and two children's, all expire on 15 March. In early December he notices his son's passport runs out in February.

  • Mid-December: he books the embassy appointment and applies for the new passport.
  • Late January: the new passport is ready. He pays a SAR 150 parking fine that was sitting on his record.
  • Early February: he updates his son's passport in Absher under Family Members in five minutes. HR confirms his own passport is still fine.
  • Late February: he pays the fees and renews all four iqamas, two weeks early. No late fine, no stress.

Late Renewal Fines

The iqama should be renewed before it expires, and the passports directorate asks residents to renew at least a few days early. Late renewal is fined SAR 500 the first time and SAR 1,000 the second, and repeated violations can lead to deportation. A slow passport renewal isn't accepted as an excuse, so start the passport process two to three months before your iqama expires.

See your deadlines in the Iqama Expiry Planner and the cost in Iqama Renewal Fees.

Passport Expiring While You're Abroad?

You can renew your passport in your home country, but the update on your iqama record has to be done while you're inside Saudi Arabia. So come back on your valid exit re-entry visa, with your iqama still valid, and travel on the new passport, carrying the old one too. Then ask HR to update the details straight away. Bring both passports to the airport so immigration can match the visa to your new document.

Mistakes People Make

  • Only checking your own passport. One child's expiring passport can hold up the whole family's renewal.
  • Leaving it to the last week. Embassy queues don't care about your iqama date.
  • Ignoring small fines. A forgotten parking ticket can block the passport update.
  • Travelling with a nearly expired passport. Your exit re-entry visa and airline both need valid travel documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I renew my iqama with an expired passport?
No. Renew the passport first, have it updated on your iqama record, then renew the iqama.
Who updates my new passport details on my iqama?
Your employer, through Absher Business or Muqeem. Workers can't update their own passport. You update your dependants' passports in your own Absher account.
What is the fine for renewing an iqama late?
SAR 500 the first time and SAR 1,000 the second time. Repeated violations can lead to deportation.
Do I need to be in Saudi Arabia to update my passport?
Yes. The person whose passport is being updated must be inside the Kingdom.
Why is Absher refusing my passport update?
Usually because of unpaid traffic fines, the person being outside Saudi Arabia, or the update being tried too early. The on-screen message says which.
