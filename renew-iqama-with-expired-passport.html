Iqama Renewal With an Expired Passport

Absher rejected your iqama renewal and the reason is your passport. Here is the order to fix it, and who has to press which button.

Updated November 2026Saudi Labour LawFor expat employees
Home › Guides › Iqama Renewal With Expired Passport

Short answer: you can't renew an iqama on an expired passport, or one about to expire. Renew the passport first at your embassy or consulate in Saudi Arabia. Then the new passport must be updated on your iqama record: your employer does this for you through Absher Business or Muqeem, and you do it yourself for your family members in Absher. Once the record shows the new passport, the iqama can be renewed as normal.

Step by Step

  1. Renew your passport at your embassy or consulate. Book early: some embassies take several weeks.
  2. Pay any traffic fines. The passport update is blocked while there are unpaid violations on the iqama.
  3. Update the passport details. For yourself, ask HR to use the "update passport information" service in Absher Business or Muqeem, because workers can't update their own passport. For dependants, use your Absher account: Family Members, then Update Passport Information.
  4. Check in Absher that the new passport number now shows on the iqama.
  5. Renew the iqama after paying the iqama fee, the levy and any dependent fees through SADAD.

Conditions to Know

  • The person must be inside Saudi Arabia when the update is done.
  • The online update is limited in how often it can be used, and in how long before the old passport's expiry you can do it. If Absher refuses, the message usually says which condition failed.
  • If the old passport was lost, not just expired, report the loss first. Updating a lost passport goes through Jawazat and carries a fine.
  • A small service fee applies to the online passport update.

Late Renewal Fines

The iqama itself should be renewed before it expires, and the passports directorate asks residents to renew at least a few days early. Late renewal is fined: SAR 500 for the first time, SAR 1,000 for the second, and repeated violations can lead to deportation. A slow passport renewal is not accepted as an excuse, so start the passport process at least two to three months before your iqama expires.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I renew my iqama with an expired passport?
No. Renew the passport first, have it updated on your iqama record, then renew the iqama.
Who updates my new passport details on my iqama?
Your employer, through Absher Business or Muqeem. Workers cannot update their own passport. You update your dependants' passports in your own Absher account.
What is the fine for renewing an iqama late?
SAR 500 the first time and SAR 1,000 the second time. Repeated violations can lead to deportation.
Do I need to be in Saudi Arabia to update my passport?
Yes. The person whose passport is being updated must be inside the Kingdom.
