Calculators give estimates, not official figures

Every calculator and guide on Saudi Utility Hub is written to help you understand and plan: how much end of service you might receive, what GOSI is deducted, what an iqama renewal could cost your family. The results are estimates built from the rules published by Saudi authorities. They are not a statement of what you are owed or what you must pay.

Your actual figure can differ because of your contract wording, your employer's payroll setup, the exact dates in your records, exemptions that apply to you, or a rule change we haven't reflected yet. Before you sign a settlement, pay a fee, resign or travel, confirm the number with the official source linked on that page or with your employer.

Not legal, financial or immigration advice

Nothing on this site is legal, financial, tax or immigration advice, and using the site doesn't create any adviser–client relationship. Labour disputes, visa refusals, deportation risk, large financial decisions and anything urgent should go to a qualified professional or the competent authority. For example, labour disputes go to the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development's dispute services, and residency matters go to Jawazat through Absher.

Not affiliated with any government body

Saudi Utility Hub is an independent website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD), Qiwa, the General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI), Absher, Muqeem, the General Directorate of Passports (Jawazat), the Ministry of Interior, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Musaned, the Premium Residency Center, the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) or any other authority. Their names appear only so we can tell you where the official rule comes from.

This site can't see your government records. It never asks for your Absher, Qiwa, Nafath or bank login, and it can't check your visa, iqama or GOSI status. If any website or message claiming to be us asks for those details, don't enter them.

How we keep pages accurate

  • Each page cites the official source for its figures and shows when it was last reviewed.
  • When a ministry announces a change to a fee, rate or rule, the affected pages are updated and their review date changes.
  • Where official sources are unclear or reports conflict, the page says so and points you to the official place to check, rather than guessing a number.

Found something out of date? Email info@saudiutilityhub.com with the page link and what the official source says, and we'll correct it. See our editorial policy for how corrections are handled.

Frequently asked questions

Are your calculator results official?

No. They are estimates based on published Saudi rules. Confirm anything binding with the official authority or your employer before acting on it.

Is Saudi Utility Hub part of HRSD, GOSI, Qiwa or Absher?

No. It is an independent website and is not affiliated with any Saudi government body.

Can I use a result from your site in a dispute with my employer?

Use it to understand your position and to ask the right questions, but rely on your contract, your official records and the HRSD dispute services for anything formal.
