Why This Site Exists

It started with a WhatsApp message: "Bhai, if I resign after four years, how much end of service do I get?" Simple question. Except HR said one number, a cousin said another, and a forum thread from 2018 said something else entirely.

That kept happening. Why is GOSI on my payslip but not my colleague's? What will renewing my family's iqamas cost this year? Can my company really keep my passport? The answers existed, but they were scattered across government portals, out-of-date blogs and second-hand advice. So we started writing them down properly, with the rule, the maths and the official link, and Saudi Utility Hub grew from there.

Who We Are

The Saudi Utility Hub Team

We're a small team based in Riyadh. We've lived and worked in Saudi Arabia for years and done the paperwork our readers are dealing with: iqama renewals with dependants, exit re-entry visas, the first payslip with a GOSI deduction, and an end-of-service settlement after resigning.

We write and maintain every calculator and guide on the site ourselves. Each page is built from the rules published by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, GOSI, Qiwa, Absher and Musaned, and shows the date it was last reviewed. You can reach us directly at info@saudiutilityhub.com.

Official Sources We Use

For anything binding, such as a payment, a deadline or a legal filing, the official source has the final word. These are the ones we build from and link to:

  • GOSI: General Organization for Social Insurance · contribution rates and benefits
  • Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development · Labour Law, wages and complaints
  • Qiwa · contracts, transfers and labour disputes
  • Absher · iqama, visas and Ministry of Interior services

What Our Tools Can, and Can't, Do

Our calculators apply the published rules to the numbers you enter. They can't see your contract, your employer's policies or your record in any government system. So if your situation has an unusual twist, such as unpaid leave, a special contract clause or a very recent rule change, use our result as your starting point and confirm the final figure with the official portal or your HR team.
