Why We Built This

Saudi Utility Hub was built to bring accurate, practical answers about Saudi employment/payroll and Iqama/visa procedures into one place. Too much of what's available online for these topics is scattered across government portals, outdated forum posts, and conflicting blog articles — some written by people with no direct experience of the processes involved. Every tool on this site exists to answer a real, recurring question that residents and employers in Saudi Arabia actually face.

Our Mission

Saudi Utility Hub was built to solve a simple problem: people working and living in Saudi Arabia need accurate, free tools for everyday employment and residency questions — and most don't exist in one place.

Whether you're calculating your GOSI deduction, figuring out your end-of-service gratuity, or checking your Iqama expiry timeline, we give you a clear estimate instantly — based on the official rules, not guesswork.

  • ✅ Based on official rules from GOSI, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, Jawazat, Absher, and Qiwa
  • ✅ No login required — every tool works instantly in your browser
  • ✅ Calculations stay on your device — we do not store what you enter
  • ✅ Rechecked when rules change — we do not promise a fixed audit schedule; see each page's own disclaimer for its most recent update

What We Cover

Saudi Utility Hub focuses on two core areas for residents, employees, and employers in the Kingdom:

  • 💼 Employment, Payroll & HR — GOSI, end-of-service gratuity, salary slips and certificates, final settlement, overtime, annual leave, probation, NOC letters
  • 🪪 Iqama, Visa & Expatriate Procedures — Iqama expiry and transfer, dependent levy, family visas, exit/re-entry, 5-year resident ID, work visa types, Nitaqat/Saudization

Our Review Approach

Each calculator and guide is built around the published rules from the relevant Saudi authority — GOSI, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, Jawazat, Absher, and Qiwa. We recheck a page when we become aware of a rate or regulation change, rather than promising a fixed audit calendar we can't guarantee. Each page's own disclaimer states when it was last substantively updated.

How Editorial Review Works

Saudi Utility Hub is edited as a single, focused publication rather than a large newsroom. To keep review consistent, tools are grouped into two focus areas:
