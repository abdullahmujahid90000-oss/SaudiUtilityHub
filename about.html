Why We Built This

Saudi Utility Hub was built to bring practical answers about Saudi employment/payroll and Iqama/visa procedures into one place. Too much of what's available online for these topics is scattered across government portals, outdated forum posts, and conflicting blog articles — some written by people with no direct experience of the processes involved. Every tool on this site exists to answer a real, recurring question that residents and employers in Saudi Arabia actually face.

Who Operates This Site

Saudi Utility Hub is independently operated. It is not a government service, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any Saudi government entity — including GOSI, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, the Ministry of Interior, Absher, or Qiwa. Those names appear on this site only as references to the public rules our tools are built from.

Who's accountable: Saudi Utility Hub is edited by the Saudi Utility Hub Editorial Team, operating from Saudi Arabia. The site does not sell goods, hold user funds, or act as a registered financial or legal service provider, so we have not published a separate business registration number or office address — content decisions and corrections are handled directly by the editorial team at info@saudiutilityhub.com.

What We Cover

Saudi Utility Hub focuses on two core areas for residents, employees, and employers in the Kingdom:

  • 💼 Employment, Payroll & HR — salary, GOSI, end-of-service gratuity, final settlement, working hours & overtime, annual leave, and probation period
  • 🪪 Iqama, Visa & Expatriate Procedures — Iqama expiry and transfer, dependent levy, exit/re-entry, family visit and family residence visas, Muqeem visa checks, and the 5-year resident ID

What "free and no login" means in practice:

  • ✅ Every tool works instantly in your browser — no account, no signup
  • ✅ Calculations run in your browser and are not sent to or stored on our servers
  • ✅ Built around the rules published by GOSI, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, Absher, and Qiwa

Each calculator and guide is built around the rules published by the relevant Saudi authority, and we recheck a page when we become aware of a rate or regulation change — rather than promising a fixed audit calendar we can't guarantee. That is a description of how we build and maintain pages, not a claim that every page has been independently fact-checked against current live government data. Where a page states a specific figure, treat it as our best current understanding, and confirm anything binding with the official source below before you rely on it.

Official Sources We Reference

For anything binding — a payment, a deadline, a legal filing — the official Saudi government source always takes priority over this site. These are the authorities our tools are built from:

  • GOSI — General Organization for Social Insurance · contribution rates, registration, and benefits
  • Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development · labour law, employment rules, complaints
  • Qiwa · employment contracts, Saudization, employer services
  • Absher · Iqama, visas, and Ministry of Interior services

Calculator Limitations

Please read before relying on any result:

Our calculators use simplified versions of published rules to produce an estimate. They are not government calculators and are not connected to any government system.

  • They may not account for every individual circumstance, employer policy, or contract term that could change your actual figure.
  • They do not verify your identity, employment record, or Iqama status against any government database.
  • Rates and rules can change; a page may lag behind a very recent regulation update.
  • Saudi Utility Hub does not provide legal, financial, immigration, or government services, and nothing on this site is legal or financial advice.
