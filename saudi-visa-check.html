🛂 Saudi Visa Check Online 2026

Check a Saudi visa on the official MOFA, Absher and Muqeem portals using your visa application number or passport number, and understand what each status means.

🏛️ Official portals only🆓 Free to check🛡️ Scam warnings

🔎 How to Check a Saudi Visa Online

Saudi Arabia offers several official portals for checking a visa, and the right one depends on what you hold. Use only government websites — none of them ask for payment to display a visa status.

🪜 Step-by-Step: MOFA Visa Enquiry

Checking a visa by application number

1. Open visa.mofa.gov.sa and choose the visa enquiry service.

2. Select "Visa Application Number" as the search type.

3. Enter the application number from your visa slip and your date of birth.

4. Enter the image code and submit. The result shows the visa type, sponsor, issue and expiry dates and the number of entries.

Checking a visa by passport number

Select "Passport Number", enter the passport number exactly as printed, choose the nationality, add the image code and submit.

Checking residency in Muqeem

Open muqeem.sa, enter the Iqama number and date of birth, and review the residency validity, sponsor and dependant records.

📖 Understanding Saudi Visa Statuses

Issued

The visa has been printed and is valid for travel within the stated period. Check both the visa validity and the permitted duration of stay — they are different dates.

Used / Entered

The visa has already been used for entry. A single-entry visa cannot be used again after this status appears.

Cancelled

The sponsor or the issuing mission cancelled the visa. A new application is required.

Expired

The validity window has passed without entry. Expired visas cannot be extended abroad; a fresh application is needed.

Not found

Usually a data-entry problem — check the application number, the nationality selection and the date format before assuming a problem with the visa itself.

Overstaying beyond the permitted duration of stay leads to fines and possible re-entry bans. Track the exit date, not just the visa expiry date.

🛡️ Avoiding Visa Check Scams

Official Saudi visa checks are always free. Treat any site that charges a fee, asks for a bank OTP, or requests a WhatsApp message to "confirm" a visa as fraudulent. Government portals use the .gov.sa or .sa domain and never contact applicants to request payment outside SADAD.

  • Never share your Absher password or OTP with an agent.
  • Check the domain carefully — mofa.gov.sa, not lookalike spellings.
  • Fees for real services are always paid through SADAD, generated inside the official portal.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check my Saudi visa status online?
Use the official MOFA visa enquiry service at visa.mofa.gov.sa with your visa application number or passport number and date of birth. Residency status is checked on muqeem.sa.
How do I check a Saudi visa by passport number?
On visa.mofa.gov.sa choose the passport-number search, enter the passport number exactly as printed, select your nationality, add the image code and submit.
Is checking a Saudi visa free?
Yes. All official Saudi visa checks are free. Any website charging a fee to display a visa status is not an official government service.
What does "visa used" mean on the MOFA portal?
It means the visa has already been used for entry to Saudi Arabia. A single-entry visa cannot be used again once this status appears.
Why does the MOFA portal say my visa is not found?
This is usually an input problem — an incorrect application number, the wrong nationality selection or a mistyped date of birth. Re-check the details before contacting the sponsor.
How do I check my Iqama and dependants?
Use muqeem.sa with your Iqama number and date of birth to view residency validity, sponsor details and registered dependants.
How do I check my exit and re-entry visa?
Log in to Absher and open the exit/re-entry service, which shows the validity period and remaining days. The exit/re-entry calculator helps plan travel dates.
What happens if I overstay a Saudi visit visa?
Overstaying results in fines and a possible re-entry ban. Track the permitted duration of stay, which can differ from the visa expiry date.

What Each Visa Status Actually Means

When you check a Saudi visa through Muqeem, Absher or the visa.mofa.gov.sa portal, the result shows one of several states. "Valid" means the visa is active and you can travel or remain as permitted. "Issued" on a new application means it is approved but has not yet been used to enter. "Expired" means the validity date has passed — entry on that visa is no longer possible. "Cancelled" or "Final exit" means the sponsor has ended the visa, and overstay fines may be accruing.

Visa Validity vs Duration of Stay

Two dates confuse most travellers. The validity is the window during which you may enter the Kingdom (for example, a one-year multiple tourist visa). The duration of stay is how long you may remain per entry or in total (90 days per year on the tourist eVisa). An exit re-entry visa likewise has an expiry date — returning after it, even by one day, can lead to refusal at the border.

Common Check Errors and How to Fix Them

  • "No record found" — usually a mistyped passport or visa number; enter digits only, no spaces, and select the correct nationality.
  • Wrong name spelling — the check matches the Latin spelling on the visa, not necessarily your passport; copy it from the visa PDF.
  • Hijri vs Gregorian dates — Absher shows both; the Hijri date governs. Use a converter if you are checking manually.
  • Sponsor-side holds — if a valid visa shows as unusable at the airport, the sponsor may have an unpaid levy or a labour-office block; only the sponsor can resolve it.
