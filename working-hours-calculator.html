⏰ Working Hours in Saudi Arabia: How Long Is Too Long?

8 hours a day, 6 in Ramadan, a break every 5 hours: the limits that protect your time, and what every extra hour should pay.

Articles 98–107 · 8 h/day · 48 h/week · 6 h/day in Ramadan · Updated November 2026
The short version: the normal limit is 8 hours a day or 48 a week, dropping to 6 a day or 36 a week in Ramadan for Muslim employees. You need a break of at least 30 minutes every 5 hours, and anything beyond the limit, plus every hour on your rest day or a holiday, is overtime: your hourly wage plus 50% of basic. [1]

Some jobs have variations. Hours can be 9 a day for certain non-continuous work, 7 a day in hazardous industries, and shift work can be averaged over up to three weeks with Ministry approval (Articles 99–100).

🌙 Ramadan: The Two Hours People Forget

During Ramadan, a Muslim employee's normal day is 6 hours. So if your company keeps everyone on 8-hour days through the month, 2 hours of every day are overtime. Over a 22-working-day Ramadan, that's 44 overtime hours. On an SAR 8,000 basic salary, that's about SAR 2,200.

📅 Your Rest Day and Public Holidays

Friday is the default weekly rest day, but an employer can set a different day for certain kinds of work after notifying the labour office. Whatever your rest day is, every hour you work on it is overtime, and so is every hour on Eid and the other official holidays.

🧮 What Your Extra Hours Are Worth

Article 107 pays each overtime hour at your actual hourly wage plus 50% of your basic hourly wage. The calculator above uses the common convention of monthly wage ÷ 30 days ÷ 8 hours.

🚫 Signs Your Hours Aren't Being Counted Properly

  • Every day is "9 to 6 plus a bit". If your real time worked is over 8 hours, the extra is overtime unless your job is in one of the approved 9-hour categories.
  • No real break. Working more than 5 hours straight without at least 30 minutes off breaks Article 101.
  • Ramadan on a normal schedule with no overtime line on the payslip.
  • Rest-day shifts "swapped" for a day off later with no pay. A day off doesn't replace overtime pay unless you agreed to it.

Keep a simple log of your start and finish times. If the hours aren't paid, file a claim on Qiwa. It's free.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are the normal working hours in Saudi Arabia?
8 hours a day or 48 hours a week under Article 98, with at least one paid rest day a week, usually Friday.
What are working hours during Ramadan?
For Muslim employees, no more than 6 hours a day or 36 hours a week. Hours beyond that are overtime.
How long is the break in a Saudi working day?
At least 30 minutes for rest, prayer and food, and you shouldn't work more than 5 hours in a row without one (Article 101).
What is the maximum time I can be kept at work?
No more than 12 hours a day at the workplace, breaks included (Article 101).
Is working on Friday overtime?
If Friday is your weekly rest day, yes. All hours on your rest day and on official holidays count as overtime.
How is overtime pay calculated?
Your actual hourly wage plus 50% of your basic hourly wage, for each overtime hour (Article 107).

What are the legal working hours in Saudi Arabia?

The standard limit is eight hours a day or 48 hours a week, reduced to six hours a day or 36 hours a week during Ramadan for Muslim workers. Some sectors and roles have their own regimes, so compare the figure this tool produces with your contract and any sector-specific ministerial decision.

How is overtime pay calculated in Saudi Arabia?

Overtime is paid at the hourly wage plus a 50% premium, based on the wage used for the relevant period. This calculator applies that premium to the hours you enter above the standard limit; it does not model GOSI or other payslip deductions.

Are Friday and weekly rest days paid in Saudi Arabia?

The weekly rest day is a paid rest day and is normally Friday unless the nature of the work requires another day, in which case the contract or work regulations should say so. Working on a rest day is treated as overtime under the rules described above.

Do working hours include break times in Saudi Arabia?

Rest and prayer breaks are generally not counted as working hours, and workers should not remain at the workplace for more than a stated maximum total of working plus break time per day. Enter only actual working hours here so the overtime estimate is not inflated.
