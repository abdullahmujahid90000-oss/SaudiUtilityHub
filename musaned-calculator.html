What hiring a domestic worker through Musaned costs

Musaned is the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD) platform for recruiting domestic workers: housemaids, drivers, cooks, nannies and similar household roles. Every recruitment goes through it, from issuing the visa to choosing a licensed office and authenticating the contract. The first-year cost has four parts:

  1. Government visa fee: SAR 2,000 per worker, paid through SADAD.
  2. Recruitment office fee: charged by the licensed office for sourcing the worker, source-country processing and the worker's flight. HRSD caps this fee for several nationalities (table below).
  3. Contract authentication: free on Musaned.
  4. The worker's salary every month, as agreed in the contract, plus anything else the contract commits you to (such as housing and food, which the employer provides).
Musaned is entirely online. There are no Musaned "service centres" to visit and no paid "contract verification" fee. If someone asks you to pay to authenticate a Musaned contract, check the request on the official platform first.

Maximum recruitment fees by nationality

HRSD sets a ceiling on what licensed recruitment offices may charge for workers from these countries. The ceilings were last revised in January 2024. An office can charge less, but not more.

Worker nationalityMaximum recruitment fee
PhilippinesSAR 14,700
Sri LankaSAR 13,800
BangladeshSAR 11,750
KenyaSAR 9,000
UgandaSAR 8,300
EthiopiaSAR 5,900

The cap covers the office's own service. It does not include the SAR 2,000 government visa fee or the worker's salary.

Worked examples

1. Housemaid from the Philippines, office charges the cap, SAR 1,500/month

Upfront: SAR 2,000 visa + SAR 14,700 office = SAR 16,700. First year with salary: 16,700 + 12 × 1,500 = SAR 34,700.

2. Worker from Ethiopia, office quotes SAR 5,500, SAR 1,000/month

Upfront: 2,000 + 5,500 = SAR 7,500. First year: 7,500 + 12,000 = SAR 19,500.

3. Office quotes above the cap

If an office quotes SAR 16,000 for a Filipino worker, that's SAR 1,300 above the HRSD ceiling. You can choose another office on Musaned or raise it with HRSD.

Step by step on Musaned

  1. Sign in to Musaned with Nafath and check you meet the requirements for the visa type (the platform checks income and other conditions automatically).
  2. Issue the domestic worker visa and pay the SAR 2,000 fee through SADAD.
  3. Choose a licensed recruitment office on the platform, and compare prices, ratings and delivery times.
  4. Agree the contract (salary, duties and rest days) and authenticate it on Musaned. Authentication is free.
  5. Track the worker's arrival, then complete the residency and medical steps the platform guides you through.

Transferring a worker who is already in Saudi Arabia

Musaned also handles moving a domestic worker from one employer to another. The new employer pays a transfer fee, which Musaned shows before payment. It's commonly reported to rise with the number of times the worker has been transferred. The worker must agree to the transfer.

Your obligations as an employer

Domestic workers are covered by the Domestic Workers Regulation, not the general Labour Law. Under it, the employer must pay the agreed salary on time (through the wage-protection channels Musaned supports), give a weekly day of rest and paid annual leave, and must not keep the worker's passport. Our end of service calculator is for Labour Law workers. Domestic workers have their own end-of-service rules under their regulation and contract.

Frequently asked questions

Is Musaned contract authentication free?

Yes. Authenticating a domestic worker's contract on Musaned is free and done online. There's no separate paid verification and no walk-in centre.

How much is the domestic worker visa fee in Saudi Arabia?

SAR 2,000 per worker, paid through SADAD after the visa is issued on Musaned.

What is the maximum recruitment fee for a Filipino housemaid?

HRSD's cap for workers from the Philippines is SAR 14,700. That covers the recruitment office's service, not the SAR 2,000 visa fee or salary.

What is the maximum recruitment fee for a worker from Ethiopia, Kenya or Uganda?

SAR 5,900 for Ethiopia, SAR 9,000 for Kenya and SAR 8,300 for Uganda, under the ceilings HRSD revised in January 2024.

Can an office charge more than the cap?

No. The caps are the maximum licensed offices may charge for those nationalities. If an office quotes more, choose another office on Musaned or report it to HRSD.

Is a domestic worker covered by the dependent levy?

No. The SAR 400 a month dependent levy is for family members on an expatriate's iqama. Domestic workers are recruited and processed through Musaned under their own rules.
