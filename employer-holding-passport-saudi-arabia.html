Can My Employer Keep My Passport in Saudi Arabia?

Short answer: not without your written consent. Here is what the rules say, and what to do if your company won't give your passport back.

Updated November 2026Saudi Labour LawFor expat employees

Short answer: no. Saudi rules prohibit employers from keeping a worker's passport, iqama or medical insurance card. The only exception is when you have asked them in writing to keep it safe for you. Even then it is yours, and you can ask for it back at any time. An employer who refuses can be fined for each worker, and you can report it through Qiwa, the HRSD app or the 19911 hotline.

This is still common practice in some companies, often presented as "policy". It isn't legal, and you don't need to accept it.

What the Rules Actually Say

The Labour Law's implementing regulations state that an employer may not keep a worker's passport or identity documents, or those of the worker's family, without the worker's consent. The ministry treats withholding documents as a violation with a fine per worker, which is raised if the employer doesn't fix it. Published penalty schedules have put this fine in the range of SAR 1,000 to 2,000 per worker.

How to Get Your Passport Back

  1. Ask in writing. Email HR: "Please return my passport, number XXXX, by [date]. I have not given written consent for the company to hold it." Keep a copy.
  2. Wait a few working days. Most companies return it once there is a written request on record.
  3. Report it. If they still refuse, file a complaint on Qiwa (look for the service for withholding personal documents) or call 19911. Attach your email.
  4. Contact your embassy if you are about to travel or it is urgent. Embassies deal with this often and can contact the employer or issue an emergency travel document.

What If I Signed Something When I Joined?

Some contracts include a clause saying the company keeps employees' passports. A blanket clause like this doesn't override the rule, because your consent has to be genuine and you must be able to withdraw it. If you once agreed in writing, you can withdraw that agreement in writing and ask for your passport back.

Domestic Workers

Housemaids, drivers and other domestic workers are covered by the separate Domestic Workers Regulation, which also says the employer must not keep the worker's passport or iqama. Complaints go through Musaned and HRSD.

Practical Tips

  • Keep a scan of your passport, iqama and visa pages in your email or cloud storage.
  • Use the digital iqama in the Absher app (Muqeem ID) for everyday ID checks, so you don't need to carry the card or hand it over.
  • Never hand over your passport as "security" for a loan or advance from your employer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it legal for my employer to keep my passport in Saudi Arabia?
No, not without your written consent. Even with consent, you can ask for it back at any time.
What is the fine for an employer who keeps a worker's passport?
The ministry fines employers per worker for withholding documents. Published penalty schedules have put this at SAR 1,000 to 2,000 per worker, raised if not corrected.
Where do I report an employer holding my passport?
On Qiwa, the HRSD app or by calling 19911. Attach the written request you sent asking for it back.
Can HR keep my passport while renewing my visa?
Yes, briefly, for the time the process needs. It should be returned once the renewal is done.
