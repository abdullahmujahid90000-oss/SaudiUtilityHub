Premium Residency products and fees

Saudi Premium Residency (sometimes called the "Saudi green card") lets a foreign national live in the Kingdom without an employer sponsor. It's run by the Premium Residency Center. There are seven products. Two are paid for purely by fee, and five depend on what you bring (a skill, an investment, a business or a property) plus a one-time SAR 4,000 fee.

ProductFeeMain condition
Unlimited DurationSAR 800,000, onceMeets the general eligibility rules; no qualifying asset needed
Limited DurationSAR 100,000 per year, renewableMeets the general eligibility rules
Real Estate OwnerSAR 4,000, onceOwns residential property worth at least SAR 4 million, valued by a TAQEEM-accredited valuer, with no outstanding mortgage
Special TalentSAR 4,000, onceExecutives and specialists in health, science, research and similar fields who meet salary and experience criteria
GiftedSAR 4,000, onceRecognised talent in culture, arts or sport
InvestorSAR 4,000, onceA qualifying investment in a Saudi business
EntrepreneurSAR 4,000, onceAn innovative start-up approved by an accredited body
The SAR 4,000 for the five specialised products is the residency fee only. The real cost is meeting the condition: the property, the investment or the job. Detailed thresholds for Special Talent, Investor and Entrepreneur are set by the Premium Residency Center and can change, so check them on its website before you apply.

Premium Residency vs regular iqama: cost calculator

The main recurring saving is the dependent levy. Iqama holders pay SAR 400 a month for each family member on their iqama. Premium Residency holders and their families are exempt. Worked examples

1. Property owner with a spouse and two children, 5 years

Real Estate Owner fee: SAR 4,000. The dependent levy on a regular iqama would be 3 × SAR 400 × 12 × 5 = SAR 72,000. Premium Residency costs SAR 68,000 less in fees over five years, but only because the family already owns a qualifying SAR 4 million property.

2. Limited Duration, single person, 3 years

3 × SAR 100,000 = SAR 300,000, with no dependent levy to offset it. Limited Duration only makes financial sense for someone who values sponsor-free status itself: the right to change jobs freely, own property and run a business.

3. Unlimited Duration, family of five, 20 years

Fee SAR 800,000. Dependent levy avoided: 4 × 400 × 12 × 20 = SAR 384,000. On fees alone the regular iqama is still cheaper by SAR 416,000. The case for Unlimited rests on permanence and the other rights, not the levy.

What Premium Residency gives you

  • Residence in Saudi Arabia without an employer or individual sponsor
  • Family members covered, with no dependent levy
  • The right to work in the private sector and change employers
  • The right to own residential, commercial and industrial property (outside Makkah and Madinah, subject to the rules)
  • Freedom to leave and re-enter without an exit re-entry visa

General eligibility

Applicants must be over 21, have a valid passport, prove financial solvency, have a clean criminal record and a recent health report, and, if inside the Kingdom, hold a valid residency. Applications are made online through the Premium Residency Center, which also charges a separate application assessment fee.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Saudi Premium Residency cost?

Unlimited Duration costs SAR 800,000 once. Limited Duration costs SAR 100,000 a year, renewable. The five specialised products (Real Estate Owner, Special Talent, Gifted, Investor and Entrepreneur) cost SAR 4,000 once, but each requires a qualifying asset, job or business.

Do Premium Residency holders pay the dependent levy?

No. Premium Residency holders and their family members are exempt from the SAR 400 a month dependent levy that iqama holders pay for each sponsored family member.

What property value qualifies for the Real Estate Owner residency?

Residential property worth at least SAR 4 million, valued by a TAQEEM-accredited valuer and fully owned, with no outstanding mortgage.

Is the SAR 4,000 fee paid every year?

For the specialised products, SAR 4,000 is a one-time fee. The residency stays valid while you continue to meet the product's condition, for example while you still own the qualifying property.

Can I apply while working in Saudi Arabia on an iqama?

Yes. Applicants inside the Kingdom must hold a valid residency when they apply. If approved, the Premium Residency replaces the employer-sponsored status.

Where do I apply?

Online through the Premium Residency Center's official website. This page is an independent guide and cannot submit applications.
