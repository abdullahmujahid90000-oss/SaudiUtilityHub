Is the Visa Still Valid? How to Check in Two Minutes

Your own exit re-entry, a family member's while they're abroad, or a new visa issued overseas: the right free portal for each, and what the result really means.

Muqeem, Absher & MOFAAlways freeUpdated November 2026
Home › Muqeem & Saudi Visa Check

Your wife is flying back from Lahore on Thursday. Or your brother's new work visa should have been issued two weeks ago. Or you are standing at the check-in counter and someone asks, "Is your re-entry still valid?" In each case, you just want a yes or no, fast.

Short answer: there is no single "Saudi visa check" website. Check your own exit re-entry visa in the Absher app. Check a family member's exit re-entry, even while they are abroad, on Muqeem's visa validity service. Check a visa that was applied for abroad (visit, work or family) on the MOFA visa portal. All three are free.

The Muqeem Check: The Quickest Way to Help Someone Abroad

Here is the situation Muqeem solves. Your son is at university in Karachi on an exit re-entry. He can't open Absher from there, and you can't see his visa in your own Absher. Muqeem's visa validity page lets you check it with just two details.

  1. Go to vv.muqeem.sa and create a free account. Verify your email and set a password. Older guides say you can check without logging in. That changed, so expect a sign-up screen.
  2. Open Visa Validity and choose Exit Re-Entry Visa.
  3. Search by the person's iqama number.
  4. Choose date of birth as the second detail, enter it, and press Check.

Green "Permitted to Enter" means you're fine. It also shows the visa type, duration, issue date and, most importantly, the return-before date. Red means that visa can't be used to come back in.

Checking a Visa Issued Abroad (MOFA)

Use this when the visa was applied for outside Saudi Arabia: a family visit visa you requested, a new employee's work visa, or one stamped through an agency.

  1. Open visa.mofa.gov.sa and choose the visa enquiry service.
  2. Search by application number, or by passport number and nationality.
  3. Add the date of birth and the image code, then submit.

You'll see the visa type, sponsor, validity dates and number of entries.

"Visa Not Found"? It's Almost Always One of These

  • Wrong nationality selected. This is the most common mistake, especially for dual nationals.
  • A space or dash copied into the passport or application number.
  • Date of birth in the wrong calendar or format. Use the Gregorian date shown on the passport.
  • Too early. A visa that is still being processed won't appear yet. Give it a day and try again.

Copy the numbers straight from the document rather than typing them from memory. That fixes most cases.

What the Status Actually Means

StatusWhat it means for you
Issued / ValidActive and usable within its dates
UsedAlready used to enter. A single-entry visa can't be used again.
ExpiredThe window has closed. An expired exit re-entry can't bring you back, and a new visa is needed.
CancelledCancelled by the sponsor or the authorities. A new application is needed.

The trap: validity vs length of stay

These two dates catch people out every week. Validity is the window in which you can enter. Duration of stay is how long you can remain once inside. A one-year multiple-entry visit visa can still limit each stay. Put the date you must leave in your phone calendar, not just the visa's expiry date.

Don't Get Caught by a Fake "Visa Check" Site

Copycat sites dress up like government portals to collect passport numbers, and worse, the OTP code sent to your phone. Three rules keep you safe:

  • Official portals end in .gov.sa or .sa: absher.sa, muqeem.sa, visa.mofa.gov.sa.
  • Checking is free. Real government fees are paid through SADAD, never by card on a random page.
  • Never share your Absher password or OTP with an "agent" or a WhatsApp contact, however helpful they sound.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I do a Muqeem visa check?
Log in to vv.muqeem.sa with a free account, open Visa Validity, choose Exit Re-Entry Visa, search by iqama number and enter the date of birth. A green result means the visa is valid, and it shows the return-before date.
How do I check my own exit re-entry visa?
In the Absher app, tap your profile, go to My Profile and then My Visa. You'll see the visa number, type and the date you must return by.
Can I check my family member's visa while they are abroad?
Yes. Muqeem's visa validity service only needs their iqama number and date of birth, so you can check it from Saudi Arabia for them.
How do I check a Saudi visa by passport number?
On visa.mofa.gov.sa, choose the passport number search, enter it exactly as printed, pick the right nationality, add the date of birth and the image code, then submit.
Is the Saudi visa check free?
Yes. Every official check is free. A site that charges to show a visa status isn't a government service.
Why does it say my visa is not found?
Usually it's the wrong nationality, a stray space in the number, or the date of birth in the wrong format. If the visa is still being processed, it won't appear yet.
