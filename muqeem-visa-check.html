Muqeem Visa Check & Saudi Visa Check 2026

How to check an exit re-entry visa on Muqeem or Absher, check a visa issued abroad on the MOFA portal, and understand what the result means.

Which Portal Should You Use?

There is no single "Saudi visa check" website. The right portal depends on what you are checking. All of them are free; any site asking you to pay to see a visa status is not official.

Muqeem Visa Check: Step by Step

Muqeem is the passports system run by Elm with Jawazat. Most of it is for employers, but its visa validity service is the easiest way to check whether an exit re-entry visa is still valid, especially when the person is outside Saudi Arabia and cannot open Absher.

  1. Go to vv.muqeem.sa. As of 2026 you need a free account: register with your details, verify your email and set a password. (The check used to work without logging in, so older guides may skip this step.)
  2. Log in and open Visa Validity.
  3. Choose Exit Re-Entry Visa, then search by iqama number.
  4. Pick date of birth as the second detail, enter it, and click Check.

A valid visa shows a green "Permitted to Enter" result with the visa type, duration, issue date and return-before date. A red result means the visa cannot be used to enter. You can check a family member's visa the same way if you have their iqama number and date of birth.

Checking a Visa on the MOFA Portal

Use this for visas issued abroad, such as a family visit visa you applied for, a new work visa, or a visa stamped through an agency.

  1. Open visa.mofa.gov.sa and choose the visa enquiry service.
  2. Search by visa application number, or by passport number and nationality.
  3. Enter the date of birth and the image code, then submit.

The result shows the visa type, sponsor, issue and expiry dates and number of entries. If it says "not found", it is almost always a typing problem: the wrong nationality, a space in the number, or the date in the wrong format.

What Each Visa Status Means

  • Issued / Valid: the visa is active and can be used within its dates.
  • Used: the visa has already been used to enter. A single-entry visa cannot be used again.
  • Expired: the validity date has passed. An expired exit re-entry visa cannot be used to come back, and a new work visa would be needed.
  • Cancelled: the sponsor or the authorities cancelled it. You need a new application.

Validity vs duration of stay

These two dates catch a lot of visitors out. Validity is the window in which you can enter. Duration of stay is how long you may remain once you are in. A one-year multiple-entry visit visa, for example, may still limit how many days you spend in the Kingdom. Track the date you must leave, not only the visa expiry.

Avoiding Visa Check Scams

Fake "visa check" sites copy the look of government portals to collect passport numbers and OTPs. A few simple rules keep you safe:

  • Official portals end in .gov.sa or .sa (absher.sa, muqeem.sa, visa.mofa.gov.sa).
  • Checking a visa is always free. Real government fees are paid through SADAD, never by card on a random website.
  • Never share your Absher password or the OTP sent to your phone with an agent or a WhatsApp contact.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I do a Muqeem visa check?
Log in to vv.muqeem.sa with a free account, open Visa Validity, choose Exit Re-Entry Visa, search by iqama number and enter the date of birth. The result shows whether the visa is valid and the return-before date.
How can I check my exit re-entry visa on Absher?
Open the Absher app, tap your profile photo, go to My Profile and then My Visa. You will see the visa number, type and the return-before date.
How do I check a Saudi visa by passport number?
On visa.mofa.gov.sa, choose the passport number search, enter it exactly as printed, select the nationality, add the date of birth and the image code, and submit.
Is the Saudi visa check free?
Yes. Every official check is free. A website that charges to show your visa status is not a government service.
Can I check my family member's visa while they are abroad?
Yes. The Muqeem visa validity service works with their iqama number and date of birth, so you can check it for them from Saudi Arabia.
Why does the portal say my visa is not found?
Usually a typing problem: the wrong nationality, extra spaces, or the date of birth in the wrong format. Copy the numbers from the visa document and try again.
