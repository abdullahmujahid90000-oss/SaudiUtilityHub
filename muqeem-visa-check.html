2️⃣ What Muqeem Is (Verified Wording Only)

We fetched muqeem.sa directly on 27 July 2026. Its own page description, published in both Arabic and English, states that the service allows organizations to review their resident workers' data and complete passport-related transactions online. It is developed by Elm in partnership with the General Directorate of Passports (Jawazat).

This is the portal's own description of itself — not a secondary summary. It positions Muqeem primarily as a tool for employers/establishments, not explicitly as a public self-check tool for individuals.

3️⃣ Public Muqeem Service vs. Business/Subscription Service

Multiple independent secondary sources describe a separate, possibly public "visa validity" check — sometimes referenced at a different address than the main portal — that may allow an individual to check certain information using an Iqama number plus a second identifier, without a full business login.

⚠️ We attempted to independently verify this directly. The main muqeem.sa interface is JavaScript-rendered and could not be inspected beyond its page description, and a direct fetch attempt of the specific address mentioned by secondary sources did not complete. We cannot confirm whether this public check currently exists, what it requires, or what it displays. Treat this as unresolved and confirm it yourself directly on the official domain.

What we can confirm is that Muqeem's main portal describes an establishment/business service, and that the National Platform and Absher separately offer individual-facing status services described in the next section.

4️⃣ What You May Be Able to Verify — By Confidence Level

An "Exit/Re-Entry Visa Status" e-service on the National Platform (my.gov.sa), described as free, in Arabic/English, for business/citizen/general public. Absher (absher.sa) exists with separate Individuals/Business/Government portals.

9️⃣ Muqeem vs. Absher

🏢 Muqeem

Per its own description: a portal for organizations/establishments to manage passport-related transactions for resident workers. Developed by Elm with Jawazat.

👤 Absher

The Ministry of Interior's e-services platform, confirmed directly, with three separate portals: Absher Individuals (citizens/residents/visitors), Absher Business (establishments), and Absher Government.

For a personal status question, Absher Individuals or the National Platform (my.gov.sa) are generally the more directly relevant starting points based on what we verified — not Muqeem's main business portal.

🔟 Visa Validity vs. Iqama/Residency Status

These are related but distinct concepts. A visa (including an exit/re-entry visa) generally governs travel permission for a specific purpose or window. Iqama/residency status concerns your ongoing legal residence in the Kingdom. Confirm each separately through the official service that covers it — do not assume one automatically confirms the other. See our Iqama Expiry Planner for organizing your own official Iqama expiry date once you have it.

1️⃣1️⃣ Exit/Re-Entry Dates vs. Iqama Expiry Dates

An exit/re-entry visa's own validity window is separate from your Iqama's expiry date — one can be valid while the other is not. Our Exit/Re-Entry Worksheet and Iqama Expiry Planner let you record each date from your own official record separately, without assuming they match.

  • Enter sensitive details only after you have independently confirmed the official government-service domain — ideally by typing it yourself rather than following a link.

  • Never provide a password, OTP code, or card information to this website, or to any site you were not certain was official before arriving there.
  • Avoid sending unredacted screenshots of official results through messaging apps. If a screenshot is genuinely necessary for official support, redact unrelated personal identifiers first where the support process permits it.
  • If you are unsure whether a link or request is genuine, do not click it — navigate to the official domain yourself instead.

    Based on what we could directly verify: Muqeem (muqeem.sa) describes itself as a tool for organizations to manage passport-related transactions for their resident workers. Absher (absher.sa), which we also fetched directly, is the Ministry of Interior's e-services platform with three separate portals: Absher Individuals (for citizens, residents, and visitors), Absher Business (for establishments), and Absher Government. For a personal status question, Absher Individuals or the National Platform (my.gov.sa) are generally the more directly relevant starting points.

    Treat this with caution. Only enter sensitive details on a domain you have independently confirmed is official by typing it yourself, never through a link you were sent.     What is Muqeem used for in Saudi Arabia?
    Muqeem is the official platform used by establishments to manage the residency records of their expatriate workers and dependents, including issuance, renewal and visa status. Individuals normally see their own record through Absher, while employers act in Muqeem.
    How can I check my visa status in Saudi Arabia online?
    Use the official government portals: Absher for the individual record, Muqeem for the employer-linked record, and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs enquiry service for visa applications. Avoid entering an Iqama or passport number on unofficial lookup sites.
    Can I check my Iqama status without an Absher account?
    Some official enquiry services allow a limited status check with an Iqama or border number, but a full record requires an authenticated Absher session. If a page offers a full record with no login, treat it as unsafe.
    Is it safe to enter my Iqama number on visa check websites?
    Only on official government domains. Third-party sites that reproduce the look of official portals are a common phishing pattern; this page deliberately links out rather than collecting any number itself.
