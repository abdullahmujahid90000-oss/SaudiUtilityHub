They say yes

Send prices the same day. Attach the PDF price list and authorisation certificate.

JazakAllahu khairan! Here is our price list and our Darussalam authorisation. Minimum first order is ___ and delivery to your country takes about ___ days. Which titles sell best in your shop? I can put together a starter pack for Ramadan.

No reply after 5 days

Send one gentle reminder, then stop. Never send a third message.

Assalamu alaikum, a gentle reminder about my message on Darussalam trade prices. Happy to send the list if useful, and no problem at all if not.

They say no or stop

Thank them, mark the lead as sent, and do not contact again.

No problem at all, JazakAllahu khairan for letting me know. I won't message again. May Allah bless your work.
