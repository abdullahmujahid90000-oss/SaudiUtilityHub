❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How often is the Saudi Utility Hub blog updated?

We add new guides and refresh existing ones as Saudi labour law, GOSI rates, Iqama fees and immigration policy change, rather than on a fixed schedule.

Are these guides official legal advice?

No. Our guides explain Saudi rules and processes in plain language for informational purposes, but they are not a substitute for official government sources or a licensed lawyer. Always verify time-sensitive details with Qiwa, GOSI, Absher or MOFA directly.

Can I request a topic for a future guide?

Yes — email info@saudiutilityhub.com with the topic you'd like covered, such as a specific labour law article, visa category or calculator scenario.
