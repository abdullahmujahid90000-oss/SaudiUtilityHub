A month is counted as 30 days, so a 45-day trip is charged as two months. The fee is per person: if your wife and two children travel with you, that is four visas. Fees are paid through SADAD and are not refunded if you cancel the visa or never use it.

Example: a single visa for 6 months costs 200 + (4 × 100) = SAR 600. A multiple visa for 6 months costs 500 + (3 × 200) = SAR 1,100.

Single or Multiple: Which One Should You Take?

For most people going home on annual leave, the single visa is the obvious choice. It is cheaper and covers one trip out and back.

The multiple visa makes sense if you will leave more than once in the same period, for example a business trip to Dubai next month and a family trip at Eid. Two single visas of two months each cost SAR 400. One multiple visa for three months costs SAR 500 and you do not have to apply again.

One thing people miss: you cannot hold two unused exit re-entry visas at the same time. If you already issued one and your plans changed, cancel it in Absher first.

How to Issue the Visa on Absher

  1. Log in to Absher (individuals). For yourself as an employee, your employer usually issues it through Muqeem or Absher Business. For your family members, you issue it from your own Absher account.
  2. Pay the fee first through SADAD in your banking app: Government Payments → Ministry of Interior (Passports) → Exit Re-Entry Visa, then enter the iqama number.
  3. In Absher, open Passports → Visas → Issue Exit Re-Entry Visa, choose the person, the visa type and the duration.
  4. Confirm, then save or print the visa. Airport immigration can see it electronically, but keeping a copy on your phone saves arguments at check-in.

Before you apply, check these: your passport should have at least 90 days of validity, your iqama must be valid, all traffic fines must be paid, any outstanding dependent fee must be paid, and the person must be inside Saudi Arabia when the visa is issued.

What If You Do Not Return in Time?

The "return before" date is a hard deadline. There is no grace period at the airport.

  • If you are outside Saudi Arabia: you cannot come back on that visa. Your employer would have to arrange a new work visa for you. The three-year entry ban that applied to people who did not return was lifted in January 2024, but you still lose your current iqama status.
  • If you are still inside Saudi Arabia and the visa expired unused, cancelling it carries a SAR 1,000 fine.

If you know you will be late, extend before the visa expires. Extensions from abroad are charged at SAR 200 per month for single visas and SAR 400 per month for multiple visas, and your iqama needs enough validity left to cover the new date. A multiple visa can only be extended once.

Documents to Travel With

  • Passport with at least six months of validity (90 days is the legal minimum for issuing the visa, but airlines and other countries often want more)
  • Your iqama or a screenshot of the digital iqama in Absher
  • A copy of the exit re-entry visa
  • A return ticket that lands before your "return before" date

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a 6-month single exit re-entry visa?
SAR 600. That is SAR 200 for the first two months plus SAR 100 for each of the four extra months. Your iqama must be valid for the whole period.
How much is a one-year multiple exit re-entry visa?
SAR 2,300: SAR 500 for the first three months plus SAR 200 × 9 extra months. Your iqama has to cover the full year.
Do my wife and children need their own exit re-entry visas?
Yes. Every family member needs a separate visa, issued by you from your Absher account, and the same fee applies to each person.
Can I extend my exit re-entry visa while I am outside Saudi Arabia?
Yes, as long as you do it before the visa expires and your iqama has enough validity. Workers usually ask the employer to extend it through Muqeem; family members can be extended by the sponsor. Extensions from abroad cost SAR 200 per month (single) or SAR 400 per month (multiple).
Can I travel if I have unpaid traffic fines?
No. Absher will not issue the visa until all traffic fines and any outstanding dependent fee are paid.
Is the fee refunded if I cancel my trip?
No. Once the visa is issued, the fee is not refunded, even if you never use it.
Who pays for my exit re-entry visa, me or the employer?
Article 40 of the Labour Law puts work-permit, iqama and exit re-entry fees on the employer for its own workers. Fees for your family members are your responsibility. Some contracts deal with annual-leave travel differently, so check yours.
