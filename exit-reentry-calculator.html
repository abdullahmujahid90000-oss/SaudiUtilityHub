Exit Re-Entry Visa: Plan Your Trip Home Without Surprises

The right visa, the right length, the right fee for every family member, plus what to do if you'll be late coming back.

Single from SAR 200Multiple from SAR 500Updated November 2026
Home › Exit Re-Entry Calculator

The tickets are booked, the suitcases are half packed, and the kids are counting down the days. The one thing that can still ruin the trip is an exit re-entry visa that's the wrong type, too short, or never issued because of an unpaid traffic fine.

Short answer: a single exit re-entry costs SAR 200 for up to 2 months, then SAR 100 for each extra month. A multiple costs SAR 500 for up to 3 months, then SAR 200 a month. Every traveller needs their own visa, your iqama must stay valid for the whole trip, and you must be back before the "return before" date.

A month is counted as 30 days, so a 45-day trip is charged as two months. The fee isn't refunded if you cancel or never use the visa.

The Khan family's summer trip

Imran, his wife and two children fly to Karachi for 6 weeks. Four single visas for 2 months: 4 × SAR 200 = SAR 800. If they'd stayed 3 months instead: 4 × (200 + 100) = SAR 1,200.

Single or Multiple? A Quick Rule of Thumb

  • One trip home on annual leave: single. It's cheaper.
  • Two or more trips in the next few months, such as a work trip to Dubai and then home for Eid: multiple. One SAR 500 visa for 3 months beats two singles at SAR 400 and saves you applying twice.
  • A 6-month assignment abroad with visits home: multiple for 6 months, SAR 500 + 3 × 200 = SAR 1,100.

You can't hold two unused exit re-entry visas at once. If your plans change, cancel the old one in Absher first.

Getting It Issued, Step by Step

  1. Check the basics: passport valid for at least 90 days, iqama valid for the whole trip, no unpaid traffic fines or dependent fees, and the traveller inside Saudi Arabia.
  2. Pay through SADAD: Government Payments → Ministry of Interior (Passports) → Exit Re-Entry Visa, then enter the iqama number.
  3. Issue it: your employer issues yours through Muqeem or Absher Business. You issue your family's in your own Absher account: Passports → Visas → Issue Exit Re-Entry Visa.
  4. Save a copy on your phone. Immigration sees it electronically, but airline check-in staff sometimes ask to see it.

Under Article 40 of the Labour Law, your own exit re-entry fee is the employer's cost. Your family's fees are yours.

Running Late? Read This Before the Date Passes

The "return before" date is firm. There's no grace period at the airport.

  • Extend before it expires. From abroad, extensions cost SAR 200 per month (single) or SAR 400 per month (multiple). Your iqama needs enough validity left, and a multiple visa can only be extended once.
  • If you miss it while abroad, you can't come back on that visa. Your employer would need to arrange a new work visa. The old three-year entry ban was lifted in January 2024, but you lose your current iqama status.
  • If the visa expires unused while you're still in Saudi Arabia, cancelling it carries a SAR 1,000 fine.

Plan the dates alongside your iqama with the iqama expiry planner. Leaving for good instead? Read final exit vs exit re-entry.

Packing List for Immigration

  • Passport with plenty of validity. Six months keeps airlines and other countries happy.
  • Your iqama, or the digital iqama in the Absher app
  • A copy of the exit re-entry visa
  • A return ticket that lands before your return-before date

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a 6-month single exit re-entry visa?
SAR 600: SAR 200 for the first two months plus SAR 100 for each of the four extra months.
How much is a one-year multiple exit re-entry visa?
SAR 2,300: SAR 500 for the first three months plus SAR 200 × 9 extra months. Your iqama has to cover the full year.
Do my wife and children need their own exit re-entry visas?
Yes. Every traveller needs a separate visa, issued by you from your Absher account, at the same fee each.
Can I extend my exit re-entry visa from abroad?
Yes, before it expires and if your iqama has enough validity. It costs SAR 200 a month for single visas and SAR 400 a month for multiple visas.
Can I travel with unpaid traffic fines?
No. Absher won't issue the visa until traffic fines and any outstanding dependent fees are paid.
Who pays for my exit re-entry visa, me or my employer?
Article 40 of the Labour Law puts exit re-entry fees for its own workers on the employer. Your family members' fees are yours.
