    🔎 Key Concepts, Explained

    Single vs. multiple entry

    A single exit/re-entry visa is generally understood to permit one trip out and back; a multiple exit/re-entry visa permits repeated trips within its validity. Exact durations and fees vary by option and are not assumed by this tool.

    Visa validity

    The period during which the exit/re-entry visa itself can be used, which sources describe as starting either from the travel date or the issuance date depending on how the duration was selected when it was issued.

    Iqama validity

    Your residency/work-permit validity, tracked separately from the exit/re-entry visa. Sources describe exit/re-entry validity as capped by remaining Iqama validity — check both directly rather than assuming one covers the other.

    "Return before" date

    The specific date by which you must be back inside the Kingdom under this visa. This worksheet's Section B treats this date as private information you already have, not something it looks up.

    Extension request

    An official service exists on the Saudi National Platform (my.gov.sa) for extending an exit/re-entry visa for a beneficiary outside the Kingdom — extension is not universally impossible, contrary to an earlier version of this page.

    Final exit

    A different, separate service from exit/re-entry, generally understood to end residency/sponsorship rather than temporarily pause it. Not something this worksheet calculates or compares in detail.

    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    Does this tool tell me my visa status or eligibility?
    No. It cannot check Absher, Muqeem, Jawazat, or any government system. It only records the fee and dates you enter yourself and does basic arithmetic on them. For your actual visa status, check Absher or Muqeem directly.
    Why doesn't this tool show a default fee?
    Reported fees vary across sources and by visa type, duration option, and traveller category, and we could not confirm a single current official fee table with confidence. Rather than guess, this worksheet asks you to enter the exact amount shown on your own official Absher/MOI/SADAD screen and confirm that it came from that screen.
    Can I extend an exit/re-entry visa?
    Yes, in at least some cases: the Saudi National Platform (my.gov.sa) lists an official service for extending an exit/re-entry visa for a beneficiary who is outside the Kingdom. This directly contradicts a claim in an earlier version of this page that extension was never possible. Whether extension applies to your specific situation should be confirmed directly through that service or Absher.
    Is there a grace period if I return late?
    Multiple Saudi service-guide sources attribute a "no grace period" rule to Jawazat, consistently enough that we note it here, but we did not independently confirm this against a primary Jawazat or Absher page in this review. Treat the return-before date on your visa as the date to act by, and verify directly if this matters to your plans.
    What happens if I don't return before the date on my visa?
    We found significantly inconsistent figures across secondary sources for overstay fines and any re-entry restriction — ranging widely and none from a primary source we could confidently cite. This worksheet does not state a specific fine amount or ban duration; contact Absher, Jawazat, or a licensed immigration professional if you are at risk of missing your return-before date.
    Do all my dependants pay the same fee?
    We did not confirm that every dependant category (spouse, children, domestic workers) is charged an identical fee. This worksheet lets you enter a separate official fee for each traveller rather than multiplying one figure automatically, so it never assumes uniform pricing.
    How is exit/re-entry visa validity different from my Iqama validity?
    They are related but separate: your Iqama is your residency/work-permit record, while an exit/re-entry visa is a temporary travel authorization layered on top of it. Sources describe the return-before date as needing to fall a number of days before your Iqama's expiry, and overall exit/re-entry validity as capped by your Iqama's remaining validity. This worksheet does not calculate that relationship for you — check both dates directly in Absher/Muqeem, and see our Iqama Expiry Planner for the Iqama side.
    Can I get an exit/re-entry visa after a final exit?
    Final exit and exit/re-entry are different services. Final exit is generally understood to end your residency/sponsorship record, so a new entry would typically require a fresh visa arranged by an employer or sponsor rather than an exit/re-entry request on a record that no longer exists. Confirm your specific situation with Absher or your sponsor.
    Is my information stored or sent anywhere?
    No. This worksheet never asks for your Iqama number, passport number, or visa number. All calculations run in your browser; nothing is sent to any server or stored in localStorage.
