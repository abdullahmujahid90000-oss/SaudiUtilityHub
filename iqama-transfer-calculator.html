    🔎 Four Different Things People Confuse

    1️⃣ A Qiwa job offer

    An initial digital offer or transfer request record created in Qiwa by a prospective new employer — the starting point, not a completed transfer.

    2️⃣ Employment-contract status

    The documented, Qiwa-registered terms of your current employment contract, including whether it has ended, is ongoing, or has a stated notice period.

    3️⃣ Employee transfer request

    The specific procedural application submitted through Qiwa to change your sponsorship/service from one employer to another — this is the actual transfer mechanism.

    4️⃣ Government residence/work-permit records

    Your Iqama/Muqeem/Absher records showing legal residency and work-permit validity — a separate system from your contractual employment relationship.

    📖 What We Could and Could Not Verify

    ⚖️ Article 40 — fee responsibility

    Consistently described across independent legal-guidance sources: the employer (specifically the new employer, for a transfer) bears the service/sponsorship transfer fee, Iqama renewal fee, profession-change fee, and exit-re-entry fee. The worker should not be made to cover these.

    Confidence: high — consistently quoted across independent legal sources

    💰 Fee schedule

    Multiple sources consistently describe an escalating fee by transfer count (commonly cited as SAR 2,000 / 4,000 / 6,000 for the 1st / 2nd / 3rd-and-later transfer), but we did not independently confirm a live, current official fee table. Enter the amount your own official Qiwa/MOI/SADAD screen shows.

    Confidence: medium — consistent across sources, not independently verified live

    🤝 Consent exceptions

    Commonly cited exception conditions include: salary unpaid for 3 consecutive months, the sponsor failing to renew an expired Iqama, a false absconding (huroob) report, the employer falling into the Red Nitaqat range, and proven unsafe/abusive conditions. Whether any applies to a specific case is not something this worksheet decides.

    Confidence: medium — reported consistently, not case-verifiable here

    🟢🔴 Nitaqat and transfers

    A Red-ranked employer is consistently reported to face restrictions on issuing/renewing work visas, and its existing foreign workers are commonly reported to gain an easier path to transfer elsewhere. Exact thresholds changed with a new "Nitaqat Developed" program phase starting April 2026 and are set by HRSD's official system, not by this worksheet.

    Confidence: medium — program details are subject to change

    ⏳ Tenure requirement

    Sources commonly cite a minimum period (around 12 months) with the current employer since first entering the Kingdom before an ordinary transfer request, unless an exception condition applies.

    Confidence: medium — reported consistently, not independently confirmed against a single primary text in this review

    We found no official processing-time commitment we could confidently cite, and reported figures vary. This worksheet makes no timing promise.

    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    Does this worksheet tell me if I'm eligible to transfer?
    No. It cannot check Nitaqat status, employer eligibility, or sponsor-consent conditions against any government system. It only organizes what you already know or see in Qiwa into a checklist of what's confirmed and what still needs official confirmation. Only Qiwa and HRSD can determine actual eligibility.
    What is Article 40 and who pays the transfer fee?
    Article 40 of the Saudi Labour Law is consistently described across independent legal-guidance sources as requiring the employer (specifically the new employer, for a service/sponsorship transfer) to bear costs including the transfer fee, Iqama renewal fee, profession-change fee, and exit-re-entry fee — the worker should not be made to pay these. This worksheet does not offer an employee/employer percentage split for this reason; enter the fee only to record what your official Qiwa/MOI/SADAD screen actually shows.
    Can I transfer without my current employer's consent?
    Multiple independent legal-guidance sources describe specific exception conditions — such as unpaid salary for 3 consecutive months, the sponsor failing to renew an expired Iqama, a false absconding report, the employer falling into the Red Nitaqat range, or proven unsafe/abusive conditions — that may allow a transfer request without consent. This worksheet cannot confirm whether any of these apply to your case; it only prompts you to check the relevant condition and confirm it in Qiwa or with HRSD.
    How much does an employee transfer cost?
    Sources consistently describe an escalating government fee schedule based on how many prior transfers a worker has had, but we did not independently verify a live current fee table, and Article 40 places responsibility on the employer, not the worker. This worksheet does not assume any default amount — enter the exact figure your official Qiwa/MOI/SADAD screen displays to you, and confirm it came from that official screen.
    What does Nitaqat have to do with my transfer?
    Nitaqat classification affects whether an employer can receive or release transfers, but the exact rules were updated with a new program phase starting April 2026 and are determined by HRSD's official system using current employer data — not by this worksheet. Confirm your new employer's current Nitaqat status directly in Qiwa.
    What if my employer hasn't paid my salary?
    Unpaid wages for an extended period is commonly cited as a condition that can support a transfer request without current-employer consent, and is also a labour-rights issue in its own right. This worksheet flags it as something to resolve or raise through Qiwa/HRSD complaint channels — it does not determine how a wage dispute affects your specific transfer.
    What happens if my Iqama or work permit has expired?
    We did not find support for treating an expired Iqama as an automatic, penalty-free, or immediate transfer right. Some sources describe the sponsor's failure to renew as one possible exception condition to sponsor-consent requirements, but this depends on your specific circumstances and is confirmed only through Qiwa/HRSD or Absher/Muqeem, not by this worksheet.
    Is my information stored or sent anywhere?
    No. This worksheet never asks for your Iqama number, passport number, employer registration number, login information, or any contract upload. All answers are processed in your browser only and are not stored in localStorage or sent to any server.
    Where do I actually submit my transfer request?
    Through Qiwa (qiwa.sa), the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development's official platform for employee transfer requests. This worksheet is a private preparation aid only and is not connected to Qiwa in any way.
