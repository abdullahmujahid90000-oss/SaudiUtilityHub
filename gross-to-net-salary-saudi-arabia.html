Gross to Net Salary: What You'll Really Take Home

Same gross salary, different take-home. See the real numbers for expats and Saudis, and what to check before you accept an offer.

No income taxExpats: 0% GOSIUpdated November 2026
Home › Salary Calculator › Gross to Net Table

Two job offers, both "SAR 12,000 a month". One is for an expat, one for a Saudi, and the amounts that actually land in the bank are different. If you've ever wondered why, or you're comparing offers right now, this page gives you the real take-home number in seconds.

Short answer: there's no income tax on salaries in Saudi Arabia. Expats take home their full gross because they pay 0% GOSI. Saudis pay GOSI on basic + housing (capped at SAR 45,000): 9.75% if they joined before July 2024, or 10.75% as a newer member (July 2026 – June 2027).

Why the Split of Your Package Matters

Most Saudi contracts split pay into basic plus allowances, typically housing at about 25% of basic and transport at about 10%. That split quietly drives several other numbers:

  • GOSI (Saudis): charged on basic + housing only.
  • Overtime: the extra 50% is calculated on basic. See the overtime calculator.
  • End of service: based on your wage, and fixed allowances usually count. See the end of service calculator.

A higher basic usually means better overtime and pension. Bigger allowances mean more cash today, so ask for the split before you sign.

Comparing Two Offers? Ask These Four Questions

  1. Is housing provided, paid as an allowance, or rolled into one "consolidated" figure?
  2. Are annual flight tickets included, and for your family too?
  3. Is there a guaranteed bonus written into the contract?
  4. Who pays the dependent fee (SAR 400 a month per family member) and family insurance?

A SAR 15,000 consolidated offer can be worth less than SAR 12,000 basic plus 25% housing, 10% transport and a written annual bonus. For expats with family, subtract the dependent fee too.

What Else Can Come Off Your Pay?

  • Repayments of company loans or salary advances
  • Unpaid leave or absence days
  • Deductions allowed by law, such as limited penalties under the company's approved work rules

Anything else deducted without a clear reason is worth questioning in writing. End of service is never a deduction from your salary. It's paid separately when you leave.

Turning Your Monthly Salary into Daily and Hourly Rates

Many payroll calculations in Saudi Arabia start from a daily or hourly rate, not the monthly figure, so it's worth knowing yours:

RateFormulaOn SAR 9,000 a month
Daily wageMonthly wage ÷ 30SAR 300
Hourly wageMonthly wage ÷ 240 (30 days × 8 hours)SAR 37.50

The daily rate is what unused annual leave is paid at when you leave, and what an unpaid absence day costs you. The hourly rate is the starting point for overtime. Check your leave value with the leave calculator, and your overtime with the overtime calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there income tax on salaries in Saudi Arabia?
No. Neither Saudis nor expats pay income tax on employment income. The standard deduction is GOSI, and only Saudis pay an employee share.
What is the net salary for SAR 10,000 gross in Saudi Arabia?
An expat takes home the full SAR 10,000. A Saudi whose whole salary is basic + housing takes home SAR 9,025 at 9.75% GOSI, or SAR 8,925 as a new entrant at 10.75%.
How much GOSI is deducted from a Saudi salary?
9.75% of basic + housing for those registered before July 2024, or 10.75% for new entrants (July 2026 – June 2027), capped at SAR 45,000 a month.
Do expats pay GOSI?
No. Expats pay 0%. The employer pays 2% for occupational hazards cover, which doesn't reduce the expat's pay.
Is housing allowance included in GOSI?
Yes. GOSI is calculated on basic salary plus housing allowance. Transport and other allowances are excluded.
Is a consolidated salary better than basic plus allowances?
Not always. Basic pay drives overtime and pension, and fixed allowances usually count for end of service. Compare the full package, including tickets, bonuses and family costs.
