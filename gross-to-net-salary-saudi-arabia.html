The short answer

Saudi Arabia levies no personal income tax on employment income. The only standard payroll deduction is GOSI (social insurance):

  • Non-Saudi employees: 0% employee deduction — your net pay equals your gross pay (basic + fixed allowances). The employer pays a 2% occupational-hazard contribution on top, not out of your salary.
  • Saudi employees: 10% employee contribution (9.75% annuities + 0.75% SANED unemployment insurance), calculated on basic salary + housing allowance, capped at a contribution ceiling of SAR 45,000/month.

What else can come off your salary?

  • Voluntary deductions: loans, salary advances, company housing or GOSI-registered deductions ordered by a court.
  • Absence penalties: unpaid leave days under the Labour Law.
  • End-of-service: not a deduction — a separate gratuity accrual under Article 84 (see the End of Service Calculator).
There is no income tax, but expats should still budget for the dependent levy (SAR 400/month per dependent) and Iqama renewal fees where the employer does not cover them.

Frequently asked questions

Is there income tax on salaries in Saudi Arabia in 2026?
No. Saudi Arabia does not levy personal income tax on employment income for Saudis or expats. The only standard payroll deduction is GOSI social insurance.
How much is deducted from salary for GOSI?
Saudi employees pay 10% of the GOSI wage base (basic + housing, capped at SAR 45,000/month). Non-Saudi employees pay nothing — only the employer contributes 2% for occupational hazard.
What is the net salary for 10,000 SAR gross in Saudi Arabia?
A non-Saudi takes home the full SAR 10,000. A Saudi employee on a typical basic+housing split pays about SAR 1,000 GOSI and takes home roughly SAR 9,000–11,000 depending on the allowance split.
Is the housing allowance included in the GOSI calculation?
Yes, for Saudi employees the contribution base is basic salary plus housing allowance (plus commissions and certain bonuses), capped at SAR 45,000 per month. Transport and other allowances are excluded.
Do expats pay any social insurance in Saudi Arabia?
Non-Saudi employees make no employee contribution. The employer pays 2% occupational-hazard insurance on their behalf, which does not reduce the expat's net pay.
