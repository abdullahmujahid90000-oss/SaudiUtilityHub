🪪 Iqama Expiry Planner: Renew On Time, Every Time

Type the expiry date from Absher and see exactly how many days you have, when to start, and what to have ready, so renewal week is calm instead of a panic.

🔒 No iqama number needed 📅 Reminder dates in one click Updated November 2026

It always seems to happen the same way: you check Absher for something else, and there it is. Iqama expires in 9 days. Then comes the scramble for the passport, the insurance, the traffic fine you forgot about, and an HR team that's on leave.

Short answer: start preparing about 90 days before your iqama expires and renew at least 2–4 weeks early. Your employer renews yours through Muqeem; you renew your family's in Absher. Renewing late is fined SAR 500 the first time and SAR 1,000 the second, and repeat offences can lead to deportation. Enter your expiry date below to get your personal timeline.

    🗓️ The Calm Way to Renew: A 90-Day Timeline

    90 days before

    Check your passport. If it has less than 6–12 months left, book an embassy appointment now, because an iqama can't be renewed on an expiring passport. Pay any traffic fines.

    60 days before

    Ask HR who is paying what, and for how long: 3, 6 or 12 months. Make sure your medical insurance is active, and your family's too.

    30 days before

    Pay the dependent fees through SADAD for your family, and check that HR has started your renewal in Muqeem.

    After renewal

    Open Absher and confirm the new expiry date for you and each family member. Check that your digital iqama has updated too.

    ⚠️ Five Things That Block a Renewal at the Last Minute

    🛂 Expiring passport

    The iqama can't outlive the passport. Renew it first, and HR then updates it in Muqeem. Here's how that works.

    🚗 Unpaid traffic fines

    Outstanding violations stop most Absher services. Clear them a few weeks ahead.

    🩺 No active insurance

    Valid medical insurance is a condition of renewal, for you and every dependant.

    👨‍👩‍👧 Unpaid dependent fees

    Your family's iqamas won't renew until the SAR 400 a month per person is paid upfront.

    📅 Hijri date drift

    The official date is Hijri, and a Hijri year is about 11 days shorter. Plan from the date in Absher, not from last year's renewal date.

    🤝 Who Renews What

    • Your own iqama (work visa): your employer renews it through Muqeem or Absher Business, and normally pays the fee and the levy.
    • Your wife's and children's iqamas: you renew them in your own Absher account after paying the dependent fees.
    • Domestic workers: renewed by the household sponsor through Absher or Musaned.

    Travelling soon? Your iqama has to stay valid for your whole trip. Plan both together with the exit re-entry planner.

    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    Where do I find my iqama expiry date?
    In the Absher app under your profile, or on your digital iqama. Employers can also see it in Muqeem. Use the Gregorian date shown there.
    How early should I renew my iqama?
    Start preparing about 90 days before expiry and renew at least 2–4 weeks early. That gives you time to fix a passport, insurance or traffic-fine problem.
    What is the fine for renewing an iqama late?
    SAR 500 the first time and SAR 1,000 the second time. Repeat offences can lead to deportation.
    Who renews my iqama, me or my employer?
    Your employer renews a work iqama through Muqeem or Absher Business. You renew your dependants' iqamas in your own Absher account.
    Can I renew my iqama if my passport is expiring?
    No. Renew the passport first. Your employer then updates the new passport in Muqeem, and the iqama can be renewed.
    Is the iqama expiry date Hijri or Gregorian?
    The official record is Hijri, shown with a Gregorian equivalent. A Hijri year is about 11 days shorter, so always plan from the date currently shown in Absher.
    Does this planner check my iqama in Absher?
    No. It only calculates dates from what you type, on your own device. It never asks for your iqama number.
