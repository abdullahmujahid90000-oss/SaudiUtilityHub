🕋 Umrah Visa Guide 2026

Everything needed to travel for Umrah in 2026 — eVisa eligibility, visa on arrival, documents, the Nusuk permit, costs and duration-of-stay rules.

🕋 Umrah Visa Options in 2026

Performing Umrah no longer requires a dedicated Umrah visa for most travellers. Saudi Arabia allows Umrah to be performed on several visa types, and the right route depends on your nationality and travel plans.

RouteWho it suitsTypical validity
Saudi eVisa (tourist)Nationals of eligible countries1 year, multiple entry, up to 90 days per visit
Visa on arrivalHolders of valid US, UK or Schengen visas/residenciesAs issued at the port of entry
Umrah visa via a licensed agentNationalities not covered by the eVisaSingle entry, defined stay

Iqama holdersResidents of Saudi ArabiaUmrah permit via Nusuk
Whatever the visa route, the Umrah permit is booked separately in the Nusuk app, which allocates the time slot for entering the Grand Mosque.

🪜 Step-by-Step Application

1. Confirm eligibility

Check whether your nationality is on the eVisa list on visa.mofa.gov.sa. If not, apply through a licensed Umrah operator in your country.

2. Prepare documents

Passport valid for at least six months, a recent photograph, confirmed accommodation, a return ticket and medical insurance (usually bundled with the eVisa fee).

3. Apply online

Complete the eVisa application, pay the fee by card, and wait for approval — commonly within a few days.

4. Book the Umrah permit

Download Nusuk, register with your passport details and reserve a permit slot for the Grand Mosque and, if desired, a Rawdah visit in Madinah.

5. Travel and verify

Carry printed and digital copies of the visa and permit. Verify the visa status on visa.mofa.gov.sa before departure.

💰 Costs to Budget For

The visa fee is only part of the cost. Plan for the eVisa fee including mandatory insurance, flights, hotels in Makkah and Madinah, intercity transport, the Umrah permit (free through Nusuk) and living expenses. Prices rise sharply during Ramadan and school holidays, so booking early materially reduces the total.

Only use licensed operators. Unlicensed "Umrah packages" advertised on social media are a common source of visa fraud — verify the operator with your local Saudi mission or the Nusuk platform.

📅 Rules, Duration and Etiquette

Duration of stay

Tourist eVisa holders may stay up to 90 days per visit within the one-year validity. Umrah visas issued through operators normally carry a shorter defined stay.

Women travelling alone

Women of any age may travel for Umrah without a mahram under current rules.

Health requirements

Check the current vaccination requirements published by the Saudi Ministry of Health before travel; requirements vary by country of origin and season.

Ihram and conduct

Enter ihram at the designated miqat, follow the Nusuk permit timing, and respect crowd-management instructions inside the Haram.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a special Umrah visa in 2026?
No. Most travellers can perform Umrah on a Saudi tourist eVisa, a visa on arrival, or a visit visa. A dedicated Umrah visa through a licensed operator is used mainly by nationalities not eligible for the eVisa.
How long can I stay in Saudi Arabia on an Umrah eVisa?
The tourist eVisa is valid for one year with multiple entries and allows a stay of up to 90 days per visit.
What documents are required for an Umrah visa?
A passport valid for at least six months, a recent photograph, confirmed accommodation, a return ticket and medical insurance, which is normally included in the eVisa fee.
What is the Nusuk permit and is it required?
Nusuk is the official Saudi platform used to book the Umrah permit time slot for entering the Grand Mosque. The permit is free and is required in addition to your visa.
Can women perform Umrah without a mahram?
Yes. Under current Saudi rules women of any age may travel for Umrah without a male guardian.
Can I perform Umrah on a visit or family visa?
Yes. Visitors already in Saudi Arabia on a visit or family visa can perform Umrah after booking a permit through Nusuk.
Can Iqama holders perform Umrah?
Yes. Residents book an Umrah permit through the Nusuk app using their Iqama details; no separate visa is required.
How much does an Umrah trip cost?
The visa fee including insurance is only part of it — flights, Makkah and Madinah accommodation, transport and living costs dominate the budget, and prices rise significantly during Ramadan.
