💼 Salary Calculator: What Actually Lands in Your Account?

Basic, housing, transport, GOSI: see exactly what you take home each month, and why two people on the same package can get different amounts.

No income tax · Expats: 0% GOSI · Saudis: 9.75% or 10.75% on basic + housing · Updated November 2026
The short version: there's no income tax on salaries in Saudi Arabia. Expats pay 0% GOSI, so take-home is usually the full gross. Saudis pay GOSI on basic + housing, capped at SAR 45,000: 9.75% if registered before July 2024, or 10.75% for newer members (July 2026 – June 2027). [1][3]

📖 What Actually Comes Off Your Salary

Here's the good news first: Saudi Arabia has no income tax on salaries. For most people, the only automatic deduction is GOSI, and whether you pay it at all depends on your nationality.

📊 Three Payslips, Three Different Stories

Ali: Saudi, long-time GOSI member, Riyadh

Basic SAR 12,000, housing SAR 5,000, transport SAR 3,000, so SAR 20,000 gross. GOSI is 9.75% of SAR 17,000 (basic + housing) = SAR 1,657.50. Take-home: SAR 18,342.50.

Noura: Saudi, first job, Dammam

Exactly the same package as Ali. Because she joined GOSI after July 2024, her rate is 10.75%: SAR 1,827.50. Take-home: SAR 18,172.50, which is SAR 170 a month less than Ali for the same job, purely because of the newer pension track.

Fatima: expat, Jeddah

Basic SAR 5,000, housing SAR 2,000, transport SAR 1,000, plus a SAR 500 extra allowance: SAR 8,500 gross. As an expat she pays no GOSI. Take-home: the full SAR 8,500, unless her employer deducts something else such as a loan instalment.

🧭 Why Your Payslip Might Not Match the Calculator

  • Loan or advance repayments from your employer.
  • Unpaid leave or absence days in that month.
  • Your employer registered a lower wage with GOSI than your contract says. Check it in the Taminaty app. A lower registered wage means a smaller pension later.
  • Housing provided instead of paid. If the company gives you a flat, GOSI counts housing at the value set by the rules, even though you don't see it in cash.

💡 Comparing Two Job Offers? Look Past the Gross

  • Basic salary matters more than it looks. Overtime is calculated from it, and a higher basic usually means more end of service too.
  • Fixed allowances count toward end of service. A SAR 2,000 housing allowance adds up over the years.
  • Family on your iqama? Subtract SAR 400 a month for each dependant. See the dependent fee calculator.
  • Housing provided vs cash. A company flat can be worth far more than a modest cash allowance in Riyadh or Jeddah.

📎 Official Sources

  • [1] GOSI — Employer FAQ — confirms the SAR 45,000 maximum contributory wage, the 9% Annuities / 2% Occupational Hazards split, that earnings subject to contribution are basic wage plus housing allowance, and that non-Saudi workers are covered only under the Occupational Hazards branch.
  • [2] GOSI — Contributor FAQ — confirms the Saned (unemployment insurance) subscription rate of 1.5% total, split 0.75% employer / 0.75% contributor.
  • [3] Royal Decree No. M/273 (Ministry of Investment of Saudi Arabia) — Clause Third of the Decree sets the Annuities-branch phase-in for new entrants at 18% combined (9% per side) for the first 12 months after the Law's effective date, rising in four annual steps to 22% combined (11% per side). With the flat 0.75% Saned rate added, this gives new entrants 9.75% in step one, 10.25% in step two, 10.75% in step three, 11.25% in step four, and 11.75% from step five onward. Independent payroll-compliance publications — including Mercans and the Saudi Compliance Institute — corroborate July 3, 2024 as the Law's effective date, which places new entrants in the 10.75% (step three) band from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.
  • [4] GOSI — Quick Verification Service — the official route to verify employment status and insurance entitlements.

The staged annual schedule for the new-entrant GOSI track comes from Royal Decree M/273 (source [3] above).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is there income tax on salaries in Saudi Arabia?
No. Saudi Arabia doesn't tax employment income. The main deduction is GOSI, and only Saudi nationals pay an employee share.
How much GOSI is deducted from an expat's salary?
Nothing. Expats pay 0%. The employer pays 2% of basic plus housing for occupational hazards (work-injury) cover.
How much GOSI do Saudis pay?
9.75% of basic plus housing for those registered before July 2024, and 10.75% from July 2026 to June 2027 for new entrants, capped at SAR 45,000 a month.
Is transport allowance included in GOSI?
No. Only basic salary and housing allowance count. Transport and other allowances are paid in full.
What is a normal housing allowance in Saudi Arabia?
Many employers pay three months' basic salary a year, which is about 25% of basic each month, but it varies by company and contract.
Why is my net salary lower than the calculator shows?
Usually loan repayments, unpaid leave, or a lower wage registered with GOSI. Check your payslip lines and the Taminaty app.

Is there income tax on salaries in Saudi Arabia?

Saudi Arabia does not levy personal income tax on employment income for residents or expatriates. The main statutory deduction from an employee's pay is the GOSI contribution, which applies to Saudi nationals; expat employees have no employee-side GOSI deduction. Other deductions on a payslip are usually contractual, such as loans, advances or voluntary insurance.

What is a good salary in Saudi Arabia for an expat family?

This depends entirely on housing, schooling and the dependent levy, which are the three largest recurring costs for a resident family. Rather than a single figure, estimate net pay here, then subtract the monthly dependent levy using our dependent levy calculator and your actual rent and school fees to see what remains.

Does my housing allowance affect my take-home pay calculation?

It increases gross pay, and for Saudi nationals it also increases the GOSI contributory base, so a higher housing allowance raises both gross and the deduction. For expat employees there is no employee GOSI deduction, so housing allowance flows through to take-home pay in full unless your contract says otherwise.

How do I calculate my daily or hourly rate from a monthly salary?

Saudi payroll practice converts a monthly wage to a daily rate by dividing by 30, and to an hourly rate by dividing that daily rate by the contractual daily hours. The same 30-day convention is used for leave payouts and end of service estimates, which is why those tools on this site use it too.
