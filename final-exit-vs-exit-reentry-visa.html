Final Exit vs Exit Re-Entry: Which Visa Do You Need?

One lets you travel and come back to the same job. The other ends your residency. Mixing them up is one of the costliest mistakes expats make.

Updated November 2026Saudi Labour LawFor expat employees
Home › Guides › Final Exit vs Exit Re-Entry

Short answer: an exit re-entry visa is for a trip. You leave, come back, and your iqama and job carry on. A final exit visa is for leaving for good. Your iqama is cancelled and your residency in Saudi Arabia ends. To come back after a final exit you need a brand-new visa, but there is no ban if you left properly.

Side by Side

PurposeHoliday, family visit, short tripLeaving Saudi Arabia permanently

ValidityThe period you pay for. Your iqama must stay valid for the whole tripUsually 60 days to leave, limited by your iqama's expiry
Who issues itYour employer (Muqeem / Absher Business); you for your dependants (Absher)Your employer, or you through Qiwa once your contract has ended
Coming backOn the same iqama, before the visa expiresOnly with a new work or visit visa

Before a Final Exit: Clear These First

  • Traffic fines and any unpaid dependent fees must be paid, or the visa can't be issued.
  • Your family on your iqama needs final exit visas too, and should leave before you or with you.
  • Loans and credit cards: settle them. Unpaid debts can lead to a travel ban through the courts.
  • Final settlement: make sure your end of service, unused leave and last salary are paid, or a complaint is filed, before you fly. See our final settlement calculator.
  • Bank account and GOSI: close or update your bank account, and download your GOSI statement and salary certificate.
If you don't leave within the final exit's validity, it can lapse and a fine can apply before it is cancelled. Book your flight as soon as it is issued.

On an Exit Re-Entry: Don't Overstay

Return before the exit re-entry visa expires. If you can't, your employer may be able to extend it online while you are abroad, but only if your iqama is still valid. Workers who failed to return used to face a re-entry ban to come back on a new work visa. The rules on this changed in 2025, so check your status in Absher before you plan around it.

Plan the dates with our exit re-entry fee calculator and iqama expiry planner.

Which One Should You Choose?

  • Going home for a holiday and coming back to the same job: exit re-entry.
  • Resigned, or your contract ended, and you have no new Saudi job: final exit.
  • Resigned with a new Saudi job lined up: neither. Your new employer transfers your iqama through Qiwa. See what happens to your iqama if you resign.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between final exit and exit re-entry?
Exit re-entry is for a trip: your iqama stays valid and you return to the same employer. Final exit ends your residency and cancels your iqama.
How much is an exit re-entry visa?
A single exit re-entry costs SAR 200 for up to 2 months, then SAR 100 per extra month. A multiple exit re-entry costs SAR 500 for up to 3 months, then SAR 200 per extra month.
How long is a final exit visa valid?
Usually 60 days to leave, limited by your iqama's expiry date.
Can I come back to Saudi Arabia after final exit?
Yes. There is no ban after a normal final exit. You need a new work or visit visa to return.
