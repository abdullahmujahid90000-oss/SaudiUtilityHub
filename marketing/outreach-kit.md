## 1. Press pitch: EOS data study

**Asset:** https://www.saudiutilityhub.com/eos-payouts-by-salary-2026.html (CSV at `/data/eos-payouts-by-salary-2026.csv`)

**Who to pitch:**

| Outlet | Desk / section | How to find the contact |
|---|---|---|
| Saudi Gazette | Local / business | Look for reporters' bylines on labour stories, or use the newsroom email in the site footer |
| Arab News | Saudi Arabia / Business | Search reporters on X or LinkedIn who covered Qiwa or labour reform stories |
| Gulf News | Business / Your Money (Gulf-wide expat finance) | Bylines on "end of service" articles |
| Khaleej Times | UAE-focused, but runs GCC comparisons | Bylines on gratuity articles |
| Pakistani expat outlets | Dawn (Business), The Express Tribune, Urdu News (urdunews.com, Saudi-based) | Overseas Pakistanis desk |
| Indian expat outlets | Gulf-focused desks at Malayala Manorama, Mathrubhumi, Onmanorama, The News Minute | Gulf / NRI desk |
| Filipino outlets | GMA News (Pinoy Abroad), Inquirer (Global Nation), ABS-CBN (OFW section) | OFW desk |

**Subject:** Data: resigning one month before 5 years can halve a Saudi worker's end-of-service pay

**Email:**

> Hi [Name],
>
> I saw your piece on [their recent article, mentioned specifically]. I thought this might be useful for a follow-up.
>
> We calculated end-of-service payouts under Saudi Labour Law Articles 84 and 85 for 8 salary bands and 9 service lengths. A few findings stood out:
>
> - A worker on SAR 10,000 who resigns at 4 years 11 months receives about **SAR 8,194**. Waiting one more month raises that to about **SAR 16,667**.
> - At the 10-year mark the jump is bigger still: about **SAR 25,500** for one extra month.
> - Workers who resign before 2 years receive nothing, while those whose fixed-term contract simply expires are owed the full award from year one, a distinction we see confused often.
>
> Full tables and a CSV are free to use with credit: https://www.saudiutilityhub.com/eos-payouts-by-salary-2026.html
>
> I'm happy to provide a comment or a custom cut (for example, typical salaries for a specific nationality or sector).
>
> Best,
> [Your name]
> Founder, Saudi Utility Hub
> [phone / WhatsApp]

**Follow up once** after 5 working days, with one new angle ("The 10-year cliff is the bigger story for long-serving workers…"), then stop.

**Timing hooks** (pitch right before these):

- Ramadan and Eid, when people resign and travel
- The end of the school year (June), when families exit
- Any MHRSD announcement about labour-law amendments
- Contract-renewal season in Q4

## 5. Next data studies (one a quarter keeps journalists coming back)

1. **"What a family really costs in Saudi Arabia, 2026."** The fixed government costs (levy, iqama, visa) by family size, plus published school-fee ranges. Use only fees you can source, and cite every one.
2. **"GOSI new-entrant phase-in: take-home pay impact 2024–2028."** Computed from Royal Decree M/273 for 6 salary levels.
3. **"Annual leave payout by salary band."** Computed from Article 109.
4. **Arabic version of the EOS study**, at `/ar/`, pitched to Okaz, Al-Madina, Sabq and Al Eqtisadiah.

Never publish survey numbers you didn't collect or "averages" you can't source. A site that gets caught fabricating data loses the links it earned, and the trust that came with them.
