### FB 1: English reply ("How much EOS for 6 years? Salary 4,500 basic + 1,125 housing")

> Brother, if your contract ends or the company terminates you:
> First 5 years = 5 × ½ = 2.5 months
> Year 6 = 1 month
> Total = 3.5 months × wage
>
> On basic only: 3.5 × 4,500 = SAR 15,750
> On basic + housing: 3.5 × 5,625 = SAR 19,687
>
> If YOU resign at 6 years, you get 2/3 of that.
>
> HRSD says fixed allowances like housing are normally part of the "wage" for EOS, so ask HR which one they used. 👍

### Forum 1: reply to "End of service calculation – am I being underpaid?"

> Worth checking two things:
>
> 1. **Which wage they used.** HRSD defines the wage for end of service as basic plus the other amounts due under your contract, so a fixed housing allowance is normally included. Many employers calculate on basic only. If your contract lists a fixed housing allowance, ask them in writing to justify leaving it out.
>
> 2. **Whether you're treated as resigned or as contract-ended.** If your fixed-term contract expired and wasn't renewed, that's Article 84 (100%), not resignation (Article 85).
>
> Formula: ½ month's wage × first 5 years + 1 month's wage × each later year (pro-rated). If you resign, take 1/3 (2–5 years) or 2/3 (5–10 years) of that.
>
> If the numbers don't match, raise it with HR first, then file through Qiwa. There's a free calculator here if it helps: https://www.saudiutilityhub.com/ksa-eos-calculator.html?utm_source=expatcom&utm_medium=community (full disclosure: I run it).

### Forum 2: reply to "Moving to Riyadh with family, budget?"

> The fixed government costs are easy to pin down, and they're the ones people forget:
>
> - Dependent levy: SAR 400/month per family member (wife + 2 kids = SAR 14,400 a year, paid upfront at iqama renewal)
> - Medical insurance per dependent (varies by plan and age)
> - Iqama issuance for each dependent
>
> Then the variable ones: rent is the biggest, and international school fees can be SAR 25k–60k per child per year. Community schools cost far less.
>
> Rough rule people use: if the package doesn't include housing and schooling, you need about 1.5× the single-person salary to live the same way with a family.
>
> Family cost calculator: https://www.saudiutilityhub.com/family-visa-cost-calculator.html?utm_source=expatcom&utm_medium=community (my site).
