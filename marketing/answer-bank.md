## End of service (EOS) and final settlement

### 1. "How much end of service will I get?"
**Short answer:** Under Article 84 you get half a month's wage for each of your first 5 years, then a full month's wage for each year after that. Partial years are pro-rated. So 5 years equals 2.5 months of wage, and 10 years equals 7.5 months. The wage is your last wage: basic pay plus fixed contractual allowances such as housing, not basic alone.
**Link:** `/ksa-eos-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 2. "If I resign, do I still get end of service?"
**Short answer:** Yes, if you have at least 2 years of service. Under Article 85, resigning gets you 1/3 of the award between 2 and 5 years, 2/3 between 5 and 10 years, and the full award after 10 years. Under 2 years you get nothing.
**Link:** `/ksa-eos-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 3. "I have 4 years 10 months. Should I wait to resign?"
**Short answer:** Almost always yes. At 5 years your share jumps from 1/3 to 2/3, which roughly doubles the payout. On SAR 10,000, resigning at 4y11m pays about SAR 8,194, while resigning at 5y pays about SAR 16,667. The same cliff happens at 10 years. Check your exact start date in Qiwa first.
**Link:** `/eos-payouts-by-salary-2026.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 4. "Is EOS on basic salary or total salary?"
**Short answer:** HRSD defines "wage" for EOS as basic pay plus the other amounts due under your contract, so a fixed housing or transport allowance is normally included. Only genuinely variable pay, such as commissions, can be excluded, and only if you agreed to that. Many employers still calculate on basic only, so ask HR in writing which figure they used.
**Link:** `/ksa-eos-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 5. "What should be in my final settlement?"
**Short answer:** Your end-of-service award, any unpaid salary up to your last day, encashment of unused annual leave, and any notice pay or compensation that applies. If your contract promised a return ticket, that too. Add each item up separately so you can see what is missing from the employer's figure.
**Link:** `/final-settlement-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 6. "My company hasn't paid my final settlement. What do I do?"
**Short answer:** The law gives the employer one week after the contract ends to pay (two weeks if you resigned). After that, file a labour complaint (amicable settlement request) through the HRSD labour-dispute service, which you can reach from Qiwa. Keep your contract, payslips and resignation acceptance. Work out the amount you're owed first so your claim has a number.
**Link:** `/final-settlement-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 7. "How do I see what EOS looks like for my salary?"
**Short answer:** We published tables of EOS payouts for 8 salary bands (SAR 3,000 to 30,000) and 1 to 20 years of service, for both termination and resignation.
**Link:** `/eos-payouts-by-salary-2026.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

## Salary, GOSI and overtime

### 8. "How much GOSI is deducted from an expat's salary?"
**Short answer:** Nothing. For non-Saudi employees the employee share is 0%, and the employer pays 2% for Occupational Hazards. If GOSI is coming out of your payslip, ask HR why, because that's often a payroll error.
**Link:** `/gosi-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 9. "What is the GOSI percentage for Saudis in 2026?"
**Short answer:** Existing subscribers pay 9% (Annuities) plus 0.75% (SANED), which is 9.75%. New entrants who joined after 3 July 2024 are on the phase-in schedule, which is 10.75% from August 2026. Contributions are calculated on basic plus housing, capped at SAR 45,000.
**Link:** `/gosi-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 10. "What will my take-home salary be?"
**Short answer:** For an expat, take-home is usually basic plus housing plus transport plus other allowances, with no GOSI deduction and no income tax. For a Saudi, deduct GOSI from basic plus housing. The salary calculator splits it all out.
**Link:** `/salary-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community` (or `/gross-to-net-salary-saudi-arabia.html` for a ready-made table)

### 11. "How is overtime calculated in Saudi Arabia?"
**Short answer:** Article 107 says overtime is the hourly wage plus 50% of the basic hourly wage. Hourly wage is monthly wage ÷ 30 ÷ 8. Work on rest days and public holidays counts as overtime too.
**Link:** `/overtime-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 12. "How many working hours are allowed? What about Ramadan?"
**Short answer:** The normal maximum is 8 hours a day or 48 a week. In Ramadan, Muslim workers drop to 6 hours a day or 36 a week. Anything above that is overtime.
**Link:** `/working-hours-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

## Leave and probation

### 13. "How many annual leave days do I get?"
**Short answer:** 21 days a year, which rises to 30 days after 5 years with the same employer. Leave can't be cashed out while you're still working there (Article 109), but any unused balance must be paid when you leave.
**Link:** `/leave-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 14. "How long can probation be?"
**Short answer:** Up to 180 days in total under Article 53, and only if it's written in your contract. Either side can end the contract during probation without EOS, unless the contract says otherwise.
**Link:** `/probation-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

## Iqama, levy and fees

### 15. "How much is the dependent fee?"
**Short answer:** SAR 400 a month for each dependent, which is SAR 4,800 a year each. It's paid in advance when the iqama is issued or renewed. So a wife and 2 kids cost SAR 1,200 a month, or SAR 14,400 a year.
**Link:** `/dependent-levy-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 16. "What is the total iqama renewal cost?"
**Short answer:** It's usually the SAR 650 iqama fee plus the SAR 800/month expat worker levy (normally paid by the employer), plus SAR 400/month for each dependent, plus medical insurance. A single worker for 12 months comes to about SAR 9,600 before insurance.
**Link:** `/iqama-renewal-fees.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 17. "My iqama expires soon. What happens if I'm late?"
**Short answer:** Late renewal brings escalating fines, and an expired iqama blocks exit re-entry, bank services and Absher services. Renew at least 2–4 weeks early. The planner shows the dates you need to act by.
**Link:** `/iqama-expiry-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 18. "How much does iqama transfer (sponsorship transfer) cost?"
**Short answer:** It's commonly cited as SAR 2,000 for the 1st transfer, SAR 4,000 for the 2nd and SAR 6,000 for the 3rd, and the new employer usually pays. Under the labour mobility rules you can move without the old employer's approval in certain cases, such as after your contract ends. Check the fee shown in Qiwa.
**Link:** `/iqama-transfer-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 19. "How much is exit re-entry?"
**Short answer:** A single exit re-entry is commonly SAR 200 for up to 2 months, plus SAR 100 for each extra month. Multiple exit re-entry costs more. Your iqama must stay valid for the whole trip.
**Link:** `/exit-reentry-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

## Family and visas

### 20. "How do I bring my family on a visit visa?"
**Short answer:** Apply on the MOFA Visa Platform, logging in through Nafath, with your iqama, your profession details and the family's passports. Fees and insurance vary. The guide walks through each step and the documents you'll need.
**Link:** `/family-visit-visa-guide.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 21. "Can I get a family (permanent) residence visa on my salary?"
**Short answer:** The family residence visa depends on your profession on the iqama and on salary rules. Budget for SAR 400/month per dependent once they arrive. The guide covers eligibility and the cost of the first year.
**Link:** `/family-residence-visa.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 22. "What does family life in KSA actually cost per year?"
**Short answer:** The dependent levy alone is SAR 4,800 per person per year. Add insurance, iqama fees and school fees on top of rent.
**Link:** `/family-visa-cost-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 23. "How do I check my visa status?"
**Short answer:** Use the MOFA enquiry with your passport number, or Muqeem or Absher if you're already resident. The guide lists which portal handles which visa type.
**Link:** `/saudi-visa-check.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community` (or `/muqeem-visa-check.html`)

## Living costs

### 24. "Why is my SEC electricity bill so high?"
**Short answer:** Residential tariffs are tiered by kWh. Once you go past the first tier, the rate jumps, so summer AC use pushes the bill up quickly. Enter your kWh to see which tier you're in.
**Link:** `/sec-electricity-bill-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`

### 25. "What's the traffic fine for X / is there a discount?"
**Short answer:** Each violation has a minimum and a maximum amount. Fines can be checked and paid through Absher, and discount schemes are announced from time to time.
**Link:** `/traffic-fine-calculator.html?utm_source=SRC&utm_medium=community`
