🪪 Iqama Renewal Fees: What You'll Actually Pay This Year

The iqama fee, the worker levy, your family's fees and insurance: who pays which, what it adds up to, and how to avoid the late-renewal fine.

💳 SADAD & Absher👨‍👩‍👧 Family fees includedUpdated November 2026

"Your iqama renewal is due." For a single worker, that message is mostly your company's problem. For anyone with a family on their iqama, it's often the biggest bill of the year, and it all arrives on one day.

Short answer: a 12-month renewal is the SAR 650 iqama fee plus the SAR 800 a month worker levy (SAR 9,600), both normally paid by your employer, plus SAR 400 a month for each family member, which you pay, plus medical insurance. A worker with a wife and two children: the employer pays about SAR 10,250, and you pay SAR 14,400 plus your family's insurance.

Article 40 of the Labour Law puts the iqama and work-permit costs on the employer. If your company deducts them from your salary without a clear clause in your contract, ask HR to explain in writing.

🧮 Three Families, Three Bills

Kumar: single, 12 months

Iqama fee SAR 650 + levy SAR 800 × 12 = SAR 9,600. About SAR 10,250 before insurance, which his employer pays.

Aisha's family: two dependants, 12 months

Employer side: SAR 10,250. Family side: 2 × SAR 400 × 12 = SAR 9,600 from her own pocket. All together: SAR 19,850 before insurance.

Tariq: tight month, 3-month renewal, one dependant

Iqama fee SAR 650 × 3/12 = SAR 162.50 + levy SAR 2,400 + dependant SAR 1,200 = SAR 3,762.50. Renewing for a shorter period spreads the cost; the monthly rate stays the same.

⚠️ The Late-Renewal Trap

Miss the expiry date and the fine is SAR 500 the first time and SAR 1,000 the second. Repeat offences can lead to deportation. An expired iqama also freezes everyday life: bank transfers, SIM top-ups and exit re-entry visas can all stop working.

Renew at least 2–4 weeks early, and much earlier if your passport is close to expiry, because the passport has to be renewed first. Get personal reminder dates from the iqama expiry planner, and work out your family's share with the dependent fee calculator.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much does iqama renewal cost in Saudi Arabia?
For a single worker renewing for 12 months: the SAR 650 iqama fee plus the SAR 800 monthly worker levy (SAR 9,600), about SAR 10,250, normally paid by the employer. Add SAR 4,800 a year for each dependant, plus insurance.
Who pays the iqama renewal fee, me or my employer?
Your employer pays the iqama fee and the worker levy for your own iqama under Article 40 of the Labour Law. You pay the SAR 400 monthly fee for each family member on your iqama, unless your contract says otherwise.
Can I renew my iqama for 3 or 6 months?
Yes. Shorter renewals are possible. The iqama fee is pro-rated, and the levy and dependant fees are charged per month, so you pay less upfront.
What is the fine for late iqama renewal?
SAR 500 for the first offence and SAR 1,000 for a repeat. A third offence can lead to deportation in serious cases.
Do I need medical insurance to renew my iqama?
Yes. A valid policy covering the renewal period must be active before the system accepts the renewal, for you and each dependant.
Where do I pay iqama renewal fees?
Through SADAD in your banking app, after the bill is generated in Absher or Muqeem. Payments usually reflect within minutes to a few hours.
