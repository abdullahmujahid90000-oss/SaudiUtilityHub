🏠 Saudi Family Residence Visa Preparation Guide

A carefully sourced preparation guide — the current official starting platform, document preparation, and a blank cost worksheet for your own figures. Family residence is conditional and renewable, not permanent. This guide does not guarantee eligibility, approval, exact cost, processing time, or in-country conversion.

🔒 No personal data collected 📋 Preparation, not approval 🧾 No fixed totals — worksheet only

1️⃣ What Family Residence Sponsorship Means

Family residence sponsorship lets an eligible resident add qualifying family members as dependents under their own residency, so those family members can live in Saudi Arabia on a dependent Iqama tied to the sponsor's status.

Not permanent: dependent residency remains conditional on the sponsor's own valid Iqama and employment, and is renewable rather than granted once and fixed forever. If the sponsor's own residency ends, dependent residency is generally affected. This guide avoids describing family residence as "permanent."

2️⃣ Four Things That Are Often Confused

👨‍👩‍👧 Family visit visa

A temporary travel authorization for a limited stay. Not residence, not work permission. Covered on our separate Family Visit Visa Preparation Guide.

🏠 Family residence entry visa

The visa/authorization that allows a qualifying family member to travel to Saudi Arabia specifically to take up dependent residency, issued after a sponsorship request is processed.

🪪 Dependent Iqama

The residency ID issued to the family member after arrival, biometrics, and Jawazat processing — this is what allows ongoing residence, tied to the sponsor.

🔁 In-country status conversion

Converting someone already inside Saudi Arabia on a different status (e.g., a visit visa) to dependent residence. We could not confirm current rules for this — see the dedicated section below.

3️⃣–4️⃣ Who May Submit a Request, and the Current Official Starting Platform

The sponsor — the resident already in Saudi Arabia — submits the request, not the family member being sponsored.

What we found: Saudi Arabia's National Platform, my.gov.sa, lists "Residence Visas" and an Iqama-renewal service (routed through Absher) among its official service directory. Multiple independent sources describe an office/platform commonly called "Istiqdam" (with a live platform at istiqdam.sa) as involved in the initial family-sponsorship request, historically via an in-person office visit and Absher for related steps. We did not confirm a single, complete, current end-to-end path from one primary source, and found inconsistent secondary-source attribution for which ministry operates the Istiqdam platform — some sources also describe "Istiqdam" mainly as a foreign domestic-worker recruitment service. Treat this as unresolved and confirm the current route directly on my.gov.sa or Absher before assuming any single path.

5️⃣–7️⃣ Sponsor, Dependant, and Relationship-Document Preparation

👤 Sponsor preparation

Current, valid Iqama; occupation checked against any permitted-profession condition; awareness that a salary-related condition is commonly reported but not confirmed at a fixed figure — the official portal determines this case by case.

👪 Dependant preparation

Valid passports for each family member (commonly reported minimum validity — confirm the current requirement on the official platform); awareness that health insurance, medical checks, and biometrics will apply after arrival.

📄 Relationship documents

Commonly reported categories: marriage certificate (spouse), birth certificate showing the sponsor's name (children). We did not confirm a universal age limit for children or a single rule for parents from a primary source — confirm your specific relationship's category on the official portal.

8️⃣ Translation and Country-Specific Attestation

Documents are commonly reported to need Arabic translation by a certified translator and an attestation chain confirming their authenticity. The exact chain (which offices, in which order) varies by the document's country of origin and was not confirmed as one universal route for every country in this review. This guide does not describe a single attestation chain that applies everywhere — confirm the chain specific to your document's issuing country with the relevant Saudi embassy/consulate or a licensed professional.

9️⃣ Application / Appointment Stages

Some sources describe an appointment or in-person office stage as part of the process; whether this applies to your case, and how it is booked, is shown by the official portal rather than assumed here. We do not provide exact menu navigation for any platform, since interfaces change over time — use the platform's own current navigation and help text.

🔟 Visa Issuance and Country-of-Origin Processing

After a sponsorship request is processed, some sources describe a confirmation document (sometimes called a "yellow slip") being issued, which the family member's home country then uses at the relevant Saudi embassy/consulate or an authorized visa-stamping service to get the visa stamped on their passport. We could not confirm this document is issued in every case, location, or form — check what your own case actually produces rather than assuming this description applies.

1️⃣1️⃣ Arrival — Insurance, Medical, and Biometrics

After arrival, dependents are commonly reported to need health insurance and to complete biometric enrollment (and, in some cases, a medical check) before a dependent Iqama is issued. Reported prices for insurance and medical checks vary considerably by source, provider, and coverage level — this guide does not state a fixed price for any of these. Record the figures your own official process shows in the cost worksheet below.

1️⃣2️⃣ Dependant Iqama Issuance

Following arrival and biometric processing, a dependent Iqama is issued through Jawazat. Reported card-issuance fees vary by source — this guide does not restate a fixed figure. Record the amount your own case shows in the worksheet below.

1️⃣3️⃣ Ongoing Dependant Levy

A separate, ongoing government charge applies to dependents in many cases. We do not restate a levy amount or a free-period claim on this page — that figure has been separately researched and verified on our dependent levy page. Use that page for the current estimate.

1️⃣4️⃣ Status Checking and Corrections

Check your sponsorship request's status directly through the official platform confirmed for your case (my.gov.sa or Absher), rather than assuming a fixed number of days for it to appear. If a confirmation document you receive contains an error, report it through the official channel that issued it as soon as you notice — this guide cannot correct or verify your documents for you.

      ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

      Is a family residence visa permanent?
      No. Family residence in Saudi Arabia is conditional on the sponsor's own residency and employment, and is renewable rather than permanent. It can end if the sponsor's own residency status ends. This guide does not describe it as a permanent visa.
      Do I apply for family residence entirely through Absher?
      We could not confirm this. Multiple sources describe an office/platform commonly called "Istiqdam" as involved in the initial sponsorship request, with Absher and the my.gov.sa National Platform involved in related residency-side services such as Iqama renewal. We did not find a single primary source confirming that the entire process runs through Absher alone, and some secondary sources gave inconsistent authority attribution for the Istiqdam platform itself. Confirm the current official route directly on my.gov.sa or Absher before assuming a single path.
      Is "Istiqdam" still the correct term for this service?
      It appears to still be in active use in 2026 across multiple independent sources, and a live platform exists at istiqdam.sa. However, sources gave inconsistent information about which ministry operates it, and some describe "Istiqdam" mainly as a foreign domestic-worker recruitment service rather than a family-sponsorship one. We are marking this as unresolved rather than stating it with confidence.
      How much does the family residence visa cost?
      We did not find a current, authoritative fee table. Multiple secondary sources mention a government fee historically near SAR 2,000, but describe it inconsistently — some as a flat family fee, others as a per-dependent charge. This guide does not restate either version as fact. Use the blank cost worksheet on this page to record the figures your own official application or portal shows, and treat those as authoritative over any number here.
      Are there fixed salary or profession requirements to sponsor family?
      Secondary sources commonly describe conditions such as a minimum Iqama validity period, an occupation within a permitted list, and a salary threshold, but we could not confirm exact current figures from a primary eligibility table. Eligibility is determined case by case by the official portal, not by a fixed number stated here.
      Can a visit visa be converted to residence status inside Saudi Arabia?
      We could not confirm this either way with reliable primary evidence, including which relationships (if any) might qualify or under what conditions. This guide does not present in-country conversion as routine or guaranteed for any relationship. If you are considering this, confirm directly with the official portal or a licensed immigration professional before relying on any outside description, including this one.
      What documents will I need, and do they need translation or attestation?
      Commonly reported categories include a salary-related document, relationship documents (such as a marriage or birth certificate), and identity documents, often requiring Arabic translation and a country-specific attestation chain. The exact list and attestation chain depend on your document-issuing country and are shown by the official platform for your case — this guide does not assume one universal chain applies to every country.
      When does the dependent levy start, and how much is it?
      This guide does not restate a levy start date or amount, because that figure is covered and separately verified on our dedicated dependent levy page. Use that page for the current dependent-levy estimate rather than any figure implied here.
      What if the official platform shows something different from this guide?
      Follow what the official platform shows you. This guide reflects patterns reported across multiple independent sources at the time of writing, not a live feed of government data. Fees, eligibility conditions, documents, and processing details can change, and the values in your own official case always take precedence over anything stated here.
      Is my personal information collected by this guide?
      No. This guide and its optional checklist and cost worksheet never ask for an Iqama number, passport number, names, birth dates, employer number, login details, or document uploads. Any answers or figures you enter are processed only in your browser and are not stored in localStorage or sent to any server.
