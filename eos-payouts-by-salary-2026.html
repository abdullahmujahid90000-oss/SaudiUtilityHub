📊 End of Service by Salary: Find Your Row, See Your Payout

Eight salary bands, nine lengths of service, termination and resignation, all worked out under Articles 84 and 85. Plus the one-month cliffs that can halve a resignation payout.

"How much will I actually get?" is the question every worker asks before resigning, and HR rarely answers it straight. So we did the maths for you: eight common salary bands, nine lengths of service, and both ways a job can end. Find your row, find your column, and you have your number.

Short answer: five years of service earns 2.5 months of your last wage, and ten years earns 7.5 months. On SAR 10,000 a month, that's SAR 25,000 and SAR 75,000 if your contract ends or you're let go. Resign instead and you get nothing under 2 years, a third from 2 to 5 years, two-thirds from 5 to 10 years, and the full award after 10.

Four Things the Numbers Reveal

  • Year six is where it speeds up. The first five years earn half a month each, and every year after that earns a full month.
  • One month can double your payout. On SAR 10,000, resigning at 4 years 11 months pays about SAR 8,194. Wait one more month and it's about SAR 16,667.
  • The 10-year mark is the biggest cliff. At 9 years 11 months a resigning worker gets two-thirds, about SAR 49,444. At 10 years it's the full SAR 75,000, around SAR 25,500 more for one extra month.
  • Under two years, resigning pays nothing. The same worker whose contract simply ends at 1 year 11 months gets about 0.96 months of wage.

Three Real Situations

Priya's contract ends after 7 years on SAR 8,000. First 5 years: 5 × 0.5 = 2.5 months. Next 2 years: 2 × 1 = 2 months. Total 4.5 months × SAR 8,000 = SAR 36,000.

Omar resigns after 3 years 6 months on SAR 5,000. Full award: 3.5 × 0.5 = 1.75 months = SAR 8,750. Resigning between 2 and 5 years pays one-third: SAR 2,916.67. If he can wait until 5 years, it's two-thirds of a bigger award.

Grace resigns after 12 years on SAR 15,000. Full award: 2.5 + 7 = 9.5 months = SAR 142,500. After 10 years a resigning worker gets the whole amount: SAR 142,500.

Mistakes That Cost People Thousands

  • Guessing your start date. Service counts from your contract start date. Check it in Qiwa before you resign near the 2-, 5- or 10-year mark.
  • Using basic salary only. The award is based on your last wage, which includes fixed allowances such as housing. Only variable items like commission can be excluded by agreement.
  • Forgetting unpaid leave. Long unpaid leave may not count as service.
  • Assuming the award includes everything. Unused leave, unpaid salary and notice pay come on top. Add them in the final settlement.
  • Resigning when the contract is about to end anyway. A fixed-term contract that simply expires pays the full Article 84 award, not the reduced resignation share. Our notice period guide explains the timing.

How We Calculated This

Formula. Article 84 gives half a month's wage for each of the first five years and one month for each year after that, with partial years pro-rated. Article 85 sets the resignation shares above. Both follow the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD) End-of-Service Award Regulations.

Wage. "Monthly wage" is the last wage used for the award: basic plus other contractual amounts, so a fixed housing allowance is normally included. The figures scale in a straight line, so you can work out any other wage by proportion.

Not included. Unpaid salary, leave encashment (see the Leave Calculator), notice compensation, Article 77 compensation for unlawful termination, and Article 80 dismissals (which carry no award).

Rounding. Tables are rounded to the nearest riyal. The full dataset (CSV) keeps two decimals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much end of service will I get after 5 years in Saudi Arabia?

If your contract ends or your employer lets you go (not for an Article 80 reason), 5 years earns 2.5 months of your last wage: SAR 25,000 on SAR 10,000 a month. Resign at exactly 5 years and you get two-thirds of that, about SAR 16,667.

Do I get end of service if I resign before 2 years?

No. Under Article 85, resigning with less than two years of service pays no award. If the employer ends the contract or a fixed-term contract expires, Article 84 applies from the first year, pro-rated.

Why does resigning one month before 5 or 10 years cost so much?

Resigning workers get one-third of the award between 2 and 5 years, two-thirds between 5 and 10 years, and the full award after 10. The share jumps at each threshold, so on SAR 10,000 resigning at 4 years 11 months pays about SAR 8,194, against about SAR 16,667 at 5 years.

Is end of service calculated on basic salary or basic plus allowances?

On the last wage, which HRSD defines as basic plus other amounts due under the contract. Fixed allowances such as housing are normally included. Only variable items like commission can be excluded by agreement.

Where do these figures come from?

They're calculated directly from Articles 84 and 85 of the Labour Law as published by HRSD. They don't include unpaid salary, leave encashment or notice pay.
