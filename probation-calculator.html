⏱️ Probation Calculator: Count Down to Day One of Job Security

Up to 180 days, Eid and sick days excluded, and either side can walk away. Here's your exact end date, and what it means for you.

⚖️ Articles 53 & 54 📏 Max 180 days Updated November 2026

New job, new city, and that quiet worry at the back of your mind: until probation ends, can they just let me go? Knowing your exact end date, and what the rules really say, takes most of that worry away.

Short answer: probation only exists if it's written in your contract, and it can't exceed 180 days in total. Eid holidays and sick leave don't count toward it. During probation either side can end the contract without compensation or end of service. Enter your start date below to see your last probation day.

    ⚖️ The Five Probation Rules That Actually Matter

    RuleWhat it means for you
    📝 It must be in writingNo probation clause in your contract means no probation period.
    📏 180 days is the ceilingYour contract sets the length, up to 180 days in total, including any agreed extension.
    🗓️ Eid and sick days don't countEid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha and sick leave push your end date later.
    🤝 Either side can walk awayDuring probation, either you or the employer can end the contract without compensation, unless the contract gives that right to only one side.
    🔁 Usually only once per employerA second probation with the same company is only allowed in writing, for a different job, or after 6 months away.

    One thing probation does not change: your employer must register you with GOSI from your first day, probation or not.

    📐 When Does My Probation End? Three Examples

    Sara: 90 days, started 1 March, with Eid in the middle

    She took 4 sick days and had the 4-day Eid al-Fitr holiday. Those 8 days don't count, so her probation ends on day 98 from her start date, not day 90.

    Arjun: 60 days, started 1 June

    No excluded days. His last probation day is 30 July. From 31 July he's past probation, and the normal notice and end-of-service rules apply.

    Lina: her contract says nothing about probation

    Then there is no probation. The company can't decide later that her first three months were a "trial".

    💡 What Ending a Contract in Probation Means

    • No end-of-service award and no compensation is owed by either side.
    • You're still owed your salary for every day worked, plus any overtime. Final dues are paid within one week.
    • Your iqama: once the contract ends, you have a limited window to transfer to a new employer or leave. See what happens to your iqama when a contract ends.
    • "Unsuitable" isn't a blank cheque. Ending a contract in probation still can't be discriminatory or a cover for something unlawful.

    🚫 Probation Myths That Catch People Out

    • "Probation is always 90 days." No. It's whatever your contract says, up to 180 days in total.
    • "They can extend it whenever they like." Only by written agreement, and never beyond 180 days in total.
    • "Annual leave doesn't build up during probation." It does. Leave builds up from day one.
    • "No GOSI until probation ends." Wrong. Registration starts on your first day.

    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    How long is the probation period in Saudi Arabia?
    Whatever your written contract states, up to a maximum of 180 days in total, including any agreed extension (Article 53).
    Do Eid holidays count towards probation?
    No. Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha leave, and sick leave, don't count. They push the end date later.
    Can my employer fire me during probation?
    Either side can end the contract during probation without compensation, unless the contract limits that right to one side. It still can't be discriminatory.
    Do I get end of service if my contract ends during probation?
    No. Neither side owes compensation or end-of-service award. You're still paid for every day worked.
    Can probation be extended?
    Only by written agreement, and the total can never exceed 180 days.
    Can I be put on probation twice with the same employer?
    Only by written agreement, and only for a different job or if at least 6 months have passed since you last worked there.
