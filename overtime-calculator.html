📋 How Overtime Is Calculated in Saudi Arabia

Under Article 107 of the Saudi Labour Law, an employer that requires an employee to work beyond normal working hours must pay an additional wage equal to the hourly wage plus 50% of the basic wage. In practice this means each overtime hour is paid at 150% of the normal hourly rate.

Step 1 — Find the monthly wage base

Add the basic salary to any allowances that are paid regularly every month. Bonuses, commissions and one-off payments are normally excluded.

Step 2 — Convert to an hourly rate

Hourly rate = monthly wage ÷ (working days per month × contracted hours per day). Most Saudi contracts use 30 days × 8 hours = 240 hours per month.

Step 3 — Apply the 150% multiplier

Overtime hourly rate = hourly rate × 1.5. Multiply by the number of overtime hours worked in the month.

Step 4 — Rest days and official holidays

Hours worked on the weekly rest day or an official holiday are also treated as overtime hours and paid at the same 150% rate, in addition to the entitlement to a compensatory rest day where applicable.

Working-hour limits: normal hours are 8 per day / 48 per week, reduced to 6 per day / 36 per week for Muslim employees during Ramadan (Article 98). Overtime is time worked beyond those limits.

🧮 Worked Example

Example 1 — Standard office employee

Basic salary SAR 6,000, no monthly allowances, 8 hours/day over 30 days = 240 hours.

Hourly rate = 6,000 ÷ 240 = SAR 25.00. Overtime rate = 25 × 1.5 = SAR 37.50.

14 overtime hours × 37.50 = SAR 525.00 overtime for the month, giving total pay of SAR 6,525.

Example 2 — Employee with allowances and holiday work

Basic SAR 8,000 + housing allowance SAR 2,000 = SAR 10,000 wage base. Hourly rate = 10,000 ÷ 240 = SAR 41.67.

10 normal overtime hours = SAR 625.05, plus 8 holiday hours = SAR 500.04. Total overtime ≈ SAR 1,125.

Example 3 — Ramadan hours

During Ramadan the normal day is 6 hours for Muslim employees. An employee working 8 hours a day accrues 2 overtime hours per day, paid at 150%.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is overtime calculated in Saudi Arabia?
Overtime is paid at the hourly wage plus 50% of the basic wage — effectively 150% of your normal hourly rate — under Article 107 of the Saudi Labour Law.
What is the hourly rate formula in Saudi Arabia?
Hourly rate = monthly wage ÷ (working days per month × contracted hours per day). A common basis is 30 days × 8 hours = 240 hours per month.
Is Friday work paid as overtime in Saudi Arabia?
Yes. Hours worked on the weekly rest day are treated as overtime hours and paid at 150% of the hourly wage.
Are allowances included in overtime pay?
Allowances that are paid regularly each month, such as housing and transport, are commonly included in the wage base. Article 107 refers to the basic wage for the 50% uplift, so check how your contract defines it.
How many overtime hours are allowed in Saudi Arabia?
Normal working hours are 8 per day and 48 per week. Total hours including overtime must remain within the limits set by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development.
How is overtime calculated during Ramadan?
Muslim employees work 6 hours a day / 36 hours a week during Ramadan. Any hours beyond that are overtime and paid at 150%.
Is overtime pay included in end-of-service gratuity?
End-of-service award is normally based on the last wage as defined in the Labour Law; irregular overtime is generally excluded. Use the end of service calculator for an estimate.
What if my employer refuses to pay overtime?
You can file a complaint through the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (hrsd.gov.sa) labour dispute service, which handles wage claims free of charge.
