⏱️ Overtime Calculator: Get Paid for Every Extra Hour

Late nights, Friday shifts, Ramadan days that run long: see what Saudi Labour Law says those hours are worth, down to the riyal.

⚖️ Article 107💵 Hourly wage + 50% of basicUpdated November 2026

You stayed until 9pm three nights this week. You came in on Friday to finish a project. Your payslip arrives, and the overtime line looks… small. Was it calculated properly?

Short answer: Article 107 says every overtime hour is paid at your normal hourly wage plus 50% of your basic hourly wage. With no allowances, that's exactly 150% of your hourly rate. Hours on your weekly rest day or a public holiday count as overtime too. Enter your figures below to see what you're owed this month.

📋 The Formula, in Three Steps

  1. Find your hourly wage. Monthly wage ÷ monthly hours. On the usual 30 days × 8 hours = 240 hours, SAR 6,000 is SAR 25 an hour.
  2. Add half your basic hourly wage. Article 107 adds 50% of the basic wage, not of the allowances. With a SAR 6,000 basic: SAR 25 + SAR 12.50 = SAR 37.50 per overtime hour.
  3. Multiply by your overtime hours, including hours on your rest day and public holidays.

If your package includes allowances, the calculator adds them to the hourly wage but applies the 50% only to basic, just as the law says.

🧮 Two Real Payslips

Nadia: office coordinator, basic SAR 6,000, no allowances

Hourly SAR 25 → overtime rate SAR 37.50. She worked 14 extra hours: 14 × 37.50 = SAR 525, so her month totals SAR 6,525.

Bilal: technician, basic SAR 8,000 + housing SAR 2,000

Hourly wage = 10,000 ÷ 240 = SAR 41.67. Extra 50% of basic hourly = 0.5 × (8,000 ÷ 240) = SAR 16.67. Overtime rate = SAR 58.33. 10 weekday hours plus 8 Friday hours = 18 × 58.33 = SAR 1,050.

🚫 Where Overtime Pay Quietly Goes Missing

  • "Your salary already includes overtime." Only if your contract clearly says so and the hours match it. Otherwise extra hours must be paid.
  • Ramadan hours counted as normal. If you work 8 hours a day in Ramadan, 2 of them are overtime.
  • Friday shifts paid at the normal rate. Rest-day hours are overtime.
  • No record of your hours. Keep a simple log of dates, start and finish times, and photos of rosters. That record is what wins a claim.

If overtime isn't paid, raise it with HR in writing, then file a claim on Qiwa. Overtime owed also belongs in your final settlement when you leave.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is overtime calculated in Saudi Arabia?
Each overtime hour is paid at your normal hourly wage plus 50% of your basic hourly wage (Article 107). If your pay is all basic, that's 150% of your hourly rate.
How do I work out my hourly rate?
Monthly wage ÷ (working days × hours per day). Most contracts use 30 days × 8 hours = 240 hours, so SAR 6,000 a month is SAR 25 an hour.
Is Friday work paid as overtime?
Yes. Hours worked on your weekly rest day or an official public holiday are treated as overtime.
Are allowances included in overtime pay?
Regular allowances are part of your normal hourly wage, but the extra 50% is calculated on basic pay. Check how your contract defines it.
How does overtime work in Ramadan?
Muslim employees' normal hours drop to 6 a day and 36 a week. Every hour beyond that is overtime.
What can I do if my overtime isn't paid?
Ask HR in writing with your hours and dates, then file a claim on Qiwa. Labour complaints are free.
