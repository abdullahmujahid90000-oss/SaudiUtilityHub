GOSI's contributory wage is basic salary plus housing allowance, capped at SAR 45,000 [1]. Existing subscribers (Saudi nationals with contribution history before the new system): 9% Annuities + 0.75% SANED = 9.75% employee; 9% + 0.75% + 2% Occupational Hazards = 11.75% employer [1][2]. Non-Saudi employees: 0% employee; employer pays only 2% Occupational Hazards [1].

📖 How to Use This GOSI Estimator

This free tool breaks down Saudi GOSI contributions by branch — Annuities, SANED, and Occupational Hazards — showing exactly what the employee pays versus what the employer pays. It is distinct from our Salary Calculator, which focuses on overall net take-home pay rather than the contribution breakdown itself.

  1. Enter Basic Salary and Housing Allowance
    These two figures make up the GOSI contributory wage. Zero housing allowance is valid.
  2. Select Employee Type
    Choose Saudi national or non-Saudi/expatriate — coverage differs by branch, not just by rate.
  3. Select GOSI Registration Status
    For Saudi nationals, choose existing subscriber or new entrant. New entrants can optionally enter their own confirmed Annuities rate if it differs from the derived default.
  4. Review the Branch-by-Branch Breakdown
    See Annuities, SANED, and Occupational Hazards for both employee and employer, plus employee/employer/combined totals.
The three GOSI branches, explained:

Annuities — the pension branch, funding retirement, disability, and survivor benefits. Paid by both employee and employer. This is the branch affected by the existing-subscriber/new-entrant distinction.

SANED — unemployment insurance. Currently 1.5% total, split evenly (0.75% employee, 0.75% employer), unaffected by registration track. [2]

Occupational Hazards — workplace-injury coverage. Paid entirely by the employer at 2% of the contributory wage, covering both Saudi and non-Saudi employees. [1]

🆕 The New-Entrant Annuities Phase-In Schedule

GOSI's own awareness platform states that the New Social Insurance System took effect on 27/12/1445H, corresponding to July 3, 2024, and that the Annuities rate for new entrants starts at 9% and increases by 0.5 percentage points per year from the system's second year through its fifth year, reaching 11% [3]. This is why the calculator lets new entrants enter their own confirmed rate as an override.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the GOSI employee contribution rate in 2026?
It depends on your registration track. Existing subscribers pay 9% for the Annuities (pension) branch plus 0.75% for SANED (unemployment insurance), totaling 9.75% of the contributory wage — both figures confirmed directly from GOSI's own published FAQ. New entrants are on a schedule that GOSI's own awareness platform describes as starting at 9% for Annuities and rising 0.5 percentage points per year from year 2 through year 5 of the new system (which took effect July 3, 2024), reaching 11%. Based on that schedule, the Annuities rate for the period spanning July 27, 2026 works out to approximately 10%, giving a total employee rate of about 10.75%. Non-Saudi employees pay 0% employee GOSI.
What is the GOSI employer contribution rate?
For Saudi national employees, the employer pays the same Annuities rate as the employee (9% for existing subscribers, or approximately 10% for new entrants as of the period spanning July 27, 2026, per GOSI's own phase-in schedule) plus 0.75% SANED plus 2% Occupational Hazards — roughly 11.75% (existing) or 12.75% (new entrant, derived) of the contributory wage. For non-Saudi employees, the employer pays only the 2% Occupational Hazards contribution; there is no employer-paid Annuities or SANED contribution for non-Saudi workers.
What's the difference between an existing GOSI subscriber and a new entrant?
GOSI's own awareness platform confirms the New Social Insurance System took effect on 27/12/1445H, corresponding to July 3, 2024, and applies specifically to workers newly joining the civil public or private sector labor market after that date who have no prior contribution period under either the Civil Pension system or the Social Insurance system. If you have any such prior contribution history, you remain under the pre-existing rules as an existing subscriber. This is a more precise definition than "registered before or after July 2024" alone — someone with prior civil-service pension history, for example, may not count as a new entrant even if they never previously had a GOSI record.
What are the Annuities, SANED, and Occupational Hazards branches?
These are GOSI's three contribution branches. Annuities is the pension branch, funding retirement, disability, and survivor benefits; it is paid by both employee and employer and is the branch affected by the existing-subscriber/new-entrant distinction. SANED is unemployment insurance, currently 1.5% total split evenly (0.75% employee, 0.75% employer), unaffected by registration track. Occupational Hazards covers workplace injury and is paid entirely by the employer at 2% of the contributory wage, covering both Saudi and non-Saudi employees.
Is housing allowance included in the GOSI contributory wage?
Yes. GOSI's own published guidance states that earnings subject to the contribution deduction are the basic wage and the housing allowance — paid in cash at the agreed amount, or valued at two months' basic salary if provided in kind — subject to the maximum contributory wage of SAR 45,000. Transport and other allowances are not mentioned as part of this base.
Does GOSI apply to non-Saudi/expatriate employees?
Non-Saudi employees are covered only under GOSI's Occupational Hazards branch, which is paid entirely by the employer at 2% of the contributory wage. They are not covered under the Annuities (pension) or SANED (unemployment) branches, so a non-Saudi employee's own GOSI deduction from their salary is 0%. This is based directly on GOSI's published FAQ distinguishing Saudi and non-Saudi coverage.
What if my basic salary plus housing allowance exceeds SAR 45,000?
GOSI's own published FAQ confirms the maximum contributory wage is SAR 45,000. Even if your basic salary plus housing allowance is higher, every GOSI branch is calculated on SAR 45,000 only.
How do I verify my own GOSI registration status and rate?
Use GOSI's own Quick Verification service to confirm your employment and insurance status directly with GOSI. We route you there rather than to Qiwa, because Qiwa is a labor-contract and employment platform, not GOSI's own contribution-verification service.
Can non-Saudi employees get a GOSI refund when leaving Saudi Arabia?
Non-Saudi employees do not have their own ongoing GOSI deduction to refund, since their coverage is limited to the employer-paid Occupational Hazards branch. GOSI and Saudi Arabia's national services platform do describe separate lump-sum benefit and end-of-service payment services tied to specific eligibility conditions, which is a distinct topic from the monthly contribution estimate on this page. We did not confirm a specific percentage or blanket "refund" entitlement for non-Saudi employees from a primary GOSI source in this review, so treat any such figure you see elsewhere with caution and confirm your own entitlement directly with GOSI or through Saudi Arabia's national services platform (my.gov.sa).
Does this calculator show my net take-home pay?
No. This page focuses only on GOSI contributions, broken down by branch and by who pays them. For an estimate of your overall net take-home pay after the employee GOSI deduction, use our separate Salary Calculator.
What happens if my employer doesn't pay GOSI contributions?
You can raise this directly with GOSI, including through its Quick Verification service and customer-care channels, and it can also be raised with the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development.
