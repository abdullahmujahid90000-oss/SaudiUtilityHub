## Part A — Ready answers

### A1. "How much end of service will I get?" (EOS / gratuity)
> End-of-service in Saudi Arabia is based on your **last full wage** (basic + fixed allowances such as housing and transport):
> - **First 5 years:** half a month's wage for each year
> - **After 5 years:** one full month's wage for each extra year
> - Part-years count proportionally
>
> **If you resign** (Article 85): nothing under 2 years, **1/3** for 2–5 years, **2/3** for 5–10 years, and the **full amount** at 10+ years. If the employer ends the contract or it expires, you get the full amount.
>
> Example: SAR 10,000 wage, 7 years → 2.5 months + 2 months = **SAR 45,000** (terminated), or **SAR 30,000** if you resign.
>
> The employer must pay within 1 week if they ended the contract, or within 2 weeks if you resigned.
>
> Free calculator (no login, nothing saved): https://www.saudiutilityhub.com/ksa-eos-calculator.html

### A2. "How much GOSI is deducted from my salary?"
> GOSI is calculated on **basic + housing allowance only** (transport isn't included), capped at SAR 45,000 a month.
> - **Saudi, existing subscriber:** 9.75% from you (9% pension + 0.75% SANED); the employer pays 11.75%
> - **Saudi, new entrant (joined after 3 July 2024):** 10.75% from you from Aug 2026 to Jul 2027 (phase-in under Royal Decree M/273)
> - **Non-Saudi:** **0%** from you; the employer pays 2% for occupational hazards only
>
> Example: basic 12,000 + housing 3,000 = 15,000 → an existing Saudi subscriber pays **SAR 1,462.50** a month.
>
> Breakdown calculator: https://www.saudiutilityhub.com/gosi-calculator.html

### A3. "What's the total cost to renew my Iqama?"
> There are three main parts:
> 1. **Iqama fee:** commonly SAR 650 per year, pro-rated if you renew for 3 or 6 months
> 2. **Expat levy (maqabil):** a monthly work-permit fee that your employer usually pays; the amount depends on the company's Saudisation band
> 3. **Dependant fee:** a monthly fee per family member under your sponsorship, paid by you
>
> Example: 1 worker + 2 dependants for 12 months at SAR 800 levy and SAR 400 per dependant → 650 + 9,600 + 9,600 = **SAR 19,850** before insurance.
>
> Always confirm the exact amount on Absher/SADAD before paying. Estimator: https://www.saudiutilityhub.com/iqama-renewal-fees.html

### A4. "Exit re-entry visa — what do I need to check?"
> Before you apply:
> - **Iqama validity:** the visa can't run past your Iqama expiry, so renew first if it's close
> - **Single or multiple:** single = one trip out and back; multiple = repeated trips within the validity
> - **Fee:** pay the exact amount Absher shows you via SADAD (it depends on type and duration)
> - **Return date:** you must be back before the "return before" date. Missing it can cause problems returning to the Kingdom, so check your status on Absher.
>
> Planner to work out your dates and record fees for the family: https://www.saudiutilityhub.com/exit-reentry-calculator.html

### A5. "How is annual leave and leave pay calculated?"
> Saudi Labour Law (Article 109): **21 days** of paid annual leave a year, rising to **30 days** after **5 years** with the same employer.
>
> Unused leave is paid out when you leave (Article 111). A common estimate is daily wage = monthly wage ÷ 30.
>
> Example: 71.6 unused days × (8,000 ÷ 30) ≈ **SAR 19,093**.
>
> Leave calculator (accrual from your start date plus payout): https://www.saudiutilityhub.com/leave-calculator.html
