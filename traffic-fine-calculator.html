How traffic fines work in Saudi Arabia

Saudi traffic fines are not single fixed amounts. The Traffic Law schedule gives each violation a minimum and a maximum fine, and the amount actually issued sits within that range. Since the 2022 amendments to the Traffic Law, a fine can also be reduced by up to 25% of its minimum value under conditions set by the Ministry of Interior, and a single fine can be paid in instalments. Your actual fine, and whether a reduction applies to it, is shown in Absher.

This calculator shows the legal range for common violations, and the lowest amount you could pay if the 25% reduction applies. It doesn't replace the amount in Absher.

The Traffic Law groups violations into categories by seriousness. The Ministry of Interior's published schedule lists Category I violations (for example driving without a licence or without lights at night) at SAR 500–900, Category II at SAR 300–500 and Category III at SAR 150–300. The most serious violations, such as running a red light or driving against traffic, carry SAR 3,000–6,000, and some also lead to the vehicle being impounded.

What about speeding?

Speeding fines are tiered by how far over the limit you were and by the road's speed limit, so there's no single figure. Published schedules start at around SAR 150–300 for the lowest band and reach SAR 1,500–2,000 for the highest. Because the bands depend on the road, check the exact amount for your violation in Absher rather than relying on a general figure.

Worked examples

1. Mobile phone use, reduction applies

Range SAR 500–900. If Absher shows the violation as eligible for the reduction, the lowest you would pay is 500 − 25% = SAR 375.

2. Two seatbelt violations, no reduction

Each is SAR 150–300, so two violations total SAR 300–600.

3. Running a red light

Range SAR 3,000–6,000. Even at the minimum less 25%, that is SAR 2,250, which is why red-light violations are the most expensive common fine.

How to check and pay a traffic fine

  1. Check: log in to Absher (Individuals) and open Traffic → Traffic Violations. Visitors can check by passport or border number through the visitors' traffic-violation query on the national portal (my.gov.sa).
  2. Pay: through SADAD in your banking app (Government Payments → Traffic Violations) using your iqama or ID number. You can pay all violations or select individual ones.
  3. Object: if you think a violation is wrong, file an objection through Absher within the period shown on the violation.
Unpaid fines can block services such as exit re-entry visas, iqama renewal and vehicle registration renewal. Check Absher before you travel. See our exit re-entry planner.

Traffic points

Alongside the fine, many violations add points to your driving record under the Traffic Points System Regulation. Too many points within a Hijri year leads to the licence being suspended, for longer periods on repeat. The number of points for each violation and your current balance are shown in Absher.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a discount on traffic fines in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Under the 2022 Traffic Law amendments, an eligible fine can be reduced by up to 25% of its minimum value, under conditions set by the Ministry of Interior. Absher shows the reduced amount when it applies. Separate one-off initiatives, such as the 2024 scheme that halved accumulated older fines, are announced by the Ministry of Interior for limited periods.

Can I pay a traffic fine in instalments?

The amended Article 73 of the Traffic Law allows a single traffic fine to be paid in split payments. Payment options appear when you pay through Absher or SADAD.

What is the fine for using a mobile phone while driving?

SAR 500 to SAR 900. Hands-free use is not the violation; holding and using the phone is.

What is the fine for running a red light?

SAR 3,000 to SAR 6,000, one of the highest common fines, and it also adds points to your record.

How do I check traffic fines as a visitor?

Visitors can check by passport or border number using the visitors' traffic-violation query on the national portal (my.gov.sa). Residents use Absher.

Do unpaid fines stop me from leaving Saudi Arabia?

Unpaid violations can block exit re-entry visa issuance and other Absher services. Pay or settle them before you plan travel.
