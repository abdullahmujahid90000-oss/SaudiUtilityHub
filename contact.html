Contact Categories

Use the email address above for any of the following:

  • Factual or calculation error — a figure, formula, rate, or statement on a tool or guide that appears wrong.
  • Broken link or technical problem — a page, button, or calculator that isn't working as expected.
  • Improvement suggestion — an idea for a new tool, guide, or a change to an existing one.
  • Accessibility problem — difficulty using the site with a keyboard, screen reader, or other assistive technology.
  • Privacy-related inquiry — a question about how the site handles information; see the dedicated section below.
  • Another legitimate matter — anything else related to the site's content or operation.

How to Report a Factual or Calculator Error

Please include:

  • The page title or URL
  • The specific statement, formula, or feature involved
  • What appeared incorrect
  • What you expected instead
  • An official source URL, if you have one
  • The approximate date you noticed the problem

For calculator problems specifically, use rounded example values, invented demonstration values, or redacted data — whatever is the minimum needed to reproduce the issue. Please do not send your real salary, employer name, residency record, or family details; a made-up example that reproduces the same result is just as useful to us and keeps your personal information out of an email inbox.

How to Report a Technical or Accessibility Problem

Please include the page title or URL, what you were trying to do, what happened instead, your approximate browser and device type (for example, "Safari on iPhone" or "Chrome on Windows laptop"), and the approximate date observed.

If you include a screenshot: crop it to only the relevant part of the page, and redact anything personal before sending. Remove names, Iqama numbers, passport numbers, visa numbers, dates of birth, employer or sponsor identifiers, account numbers, salary slips, contracts, OTP codes, passwords, QR codes, barcodes, card details, addresses, photographs of identity documents, or any other unrelated personal information.

Where possible, a short text description or an invented demonstration value is preferable to a screenshot.

Privacy-Related Inquiries

For a question about how this site handles information, email info@saudiutilityhub.com with the subject "Privacy request" and describe what you're asking about. See our Privacy Policy for more detail. This page does not restate specific legal response deadlines — refer to the Privacy Policy for how privacy requests are handled.

What Information to Include

In general, the most useful reports include a page title or URL, the specific item involved, what you expected versus what you saw, and (where relevant) an official source link, browser/device type, and an approximate date.

What Information to Never Send

Saudi Utility Hub will never need any of the following, and you should never send them by email:
  • Passwords
  • OTP or verification codes
  • Absher, Qiwa, Muqeem, GOSI, or banking login information
  • Iqama or passport numbers
  • Full identity-document images
  • Bank or payment-card data
  • Complete employment contracts
  • Medical records
  • Unredacted salary slips
  • Government-account screenshots

Please do not send urgent or highly sensitive case information by email. This inbox is not equipped to handle time-critical government, legal, medical, or financial matters — see below for where to direct those instead.

What Saudi Utility Hub Cannot Do

Saudi Utility Hub is independently operated. It is not a Saudi government service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for GOSI, HRSD, MOI, Jawazat, Absher, Muqeem, or Qiwa. As a result, this inbox cannot:

  • Access government records
  • Check an individual's application or account
  • Renew an Iqama or visa
  • Correct an Absher, Muqeem, Qiwa, GOSI, or Jawazat record
  • Contact a government authority on your behalf
  • Expedite a government application
  • Resolve an employment dispute
  • Provide personalized legal, immigration, financial, or payroll advice
  • Accept payments for government services
  • Receive passwords, OTPs, or identity documents

If your situation is account-specific, please contact the relevant official service directly — for example Absher, Qiwa, Muqeem, GOSI, or Jawazat — rather than this inbox. We have not independently verified a specific phone number or procedure for each of those services, so none is listed here; use the official channel each service itself publishes.

Correction-Review Process

  1. The message is reviewed when capacity allows.
  2. The page and any cited source are checked.
  3. If a correction is supported, the page may be updated.
  4. Material changes are reflected transparently where practical.
  5. The sender may not always receive an individual response.
  6. No resolution time is guaranteed.

This is a modest review process, not independent professional fact-checking, a fixed audit schedule, or a legal or government review.

Response Expectations

Email is reviewed as capacity allows. Response times vary, and a response is not guaranteed. Urgent government, employment, immigration, financial, or legal matters should be directed to the appropriate official authority or a qualified professional — this inbox is not an emergency or official-support channel.
