Notice Period Rules in Saudi Arabia

The 2025 Labour Law amendments changed how much notice each side must give. Here is what applies to you, and what happens if someone doesn't give it.

Updated November 2026Saudi Labour LawFor expat employees
Home › Guides › Notice Period Rules

Short answer: on an open-ended (unlimited) contract with a monthly wage, the minimum notice is 30 days if you resign and 60 days if the employer ends the contract. For workers not paid monthly, it is 30 days from either side. Your contract can set a longer notice period. If either side doesn't give the notice, it owes the other the wage for the notice period.

Who Gives How Much Notice

SituationMinimum notice
You resign (open-ended contract, monthly wage)30 days
Employer ends the contract (open-ended, monthly wage)60 days
Worker not paid monthly30 days from either side
Fixed-term contractNo notice period. Ending it early can mean paying compensation instead (see below)

Resigning: The Acceptance Rules

  • Your employer has 30 days to respond to your written resignation. Silence counts as acceptance.
  • The employer can postpone it once, by up to 60 days, for a genuine work reason given to you in writing within those 30 days.
  • You can withdraw your resignation within 7 days, as long as the employer hasn't already accepted it.

For the iqama side of resigning, read what happens to your iqama if you resign.

If Notice Isn't Given: Pay in Lieu

A side that ends the contract without the required notice must pay the other the wage for the notice period. Example: your employer ends your open-ended contract with immediate effect, and your wage is SAR 7,500. They owe you 60 days' notice pay, which is SAR 15,000, on top of your end-of-service award and other dues.

The same applies the other way: walking out without serving 30 days' notice can let the employer deduct or claim a month's wage.

Fixed-Term Contracts: Compensation, Not Notice

Fixed-term contracts end on their end date, so there's no notice period in the usual sense. If one side ends a fixed-term contract early without a lawful reason, it may owe compensation, typically the wages for the remaining period, unless the contract sets a different amount. Read the termination clause before you resign mid-term.

During the Notice Period

  • You are still employed: full salary, GOSI and service time all continue.
  • Your end-of-service service period includes the notice period.
  • Use the time to settle your final dues in writing. Check them with our final settlement calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the notice period in Saudi Arabia?
For an open-ended contract with a monthly wage, at least 30 days if the worker resigns and 60 days if the employer ends the contract. For workers not paid monthly, 30 days from either side.
Can my employer refuse my resignation?
Not indefinitely. They have 30 days to respond, and silence counts as acceptance. They can postpone it once by up to 60 days for a genuine work reason given in writing.
What if my employer fires me without notice?
They owe you the wage for the 60-day notice period, in addition to your end of service and other dues.
Is there a notice period on a fixed-term contract?
Not in the usual sense. Ending a fixed-term contract early without a lawful reason can require compensation instead.
