Who runs this site, and how is it reviewed?

Saudi Utility Hub is independently operated and not affiliated with any Saudi government body. Tools and guides are built around the rules published by the relevant authority — GOSI, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, and Absher/Jawazat/Muqeem — and are reviewed when we become aware of a material rate, regulation, or procedure change, rather than on a fixed schedule. This is not a promise of continuous, scheduled, or professional fact-checking; official sources control for anything binding. Read more on our About page.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Saudi Utility Hub really free to use?

Yes. Every calculator and guide on this site is completely free, with no login, signup, or paid tier. Advertising is not currently active on this site; if it is added in the future, after AdSense approval and appropriate privacy preparation, the tools will remain free and will not require an account.

What does Saudi Utility Hub cover?

Two areas only: employment, payroll and HR tools (salary, GOSI, end-of-service, final settlement, working hours, leave, and probation), and Iqama, visa and residency preparation guides (Iqama renewal and transfer, dependent levy, exit/re-entry, family and visit visas, and the 5-year resident ID).

Do I need to create an account to use these tools?

No account or login is required for any calculator on Saudi Utility Hub. Every tool runs instantly in your browser.

Is the data I enter stored or shared?

No. Calculations run locally in your browser. We do not send the numbers you enter (salary, dates, etc.) to our servers or store them.

How often are the tools and guides updated?

Pages are reviewed when we become aware of a material rate, regulation, or procedure change — not on a fixed schedule. This is not a promise of continuous, scheduled, or professional fact-checking. For anything binding, confirm the figure with the official source directly.

Which tool should I start with?

New expats often start with the Iqama Expiry Date Planner. Employees checking their pay usually start with the Salary or GOSI calculator.
