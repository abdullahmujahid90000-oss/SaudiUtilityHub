Three Numbers Every Expat Should Know

SAR 400 a month. The dependent fee for each family member on your iqama: SAR 4,800 a year per person, paid at every renewal.

Five years. The end-of-service mark where a resignation payout jumps from one-third to two-thirds of the award. On SAR 10,000 a month, resigning at 4 years 11 months pays about SAR 8,194. One month later it's about SAR 16,667.

SAR 500. The fine for renewing an iqama late the first time, rising to SAR 1,000 the second time. A reminder 30 days before expiry costs nothing.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Saudi Utility Hub free to use?

Yes. Every calculator and guide is free, with no login, sign-up or paid tier. Advertising pays for the site and never changes how a calculator works or what a guide says.

Is the data I enter stored or shared?

No. Calculations run in your browser, and the salaries, dates and other figures you type are never sent to us or stored. The Privacy Policy explains how analytics and advertising cookies work and how to turn them off.

Which calculator should I use for my end of service?

The End of Service Calculator gives you the gratuity on its own. The Final Settlement Calculator adds unpaid salary, unused leave and deductions to give you one exit figure.

How is GOSI deducted from a Saudi salary?

For Saudi nationals, the employee share is worked out on basic salary plus housing, capped at SAR 45,000 a month. Existing subscribers pay 9.75%. New entrants pay 10.75% from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027 under the phase-in. Expats pay no employee share; the employer pays 2% for occupational hazards.

Is there income tax on salaries in Saudi Arabia?

No. Saudi Arabia doesn't tax employment income, for Saudis or expats. For Saudis the main deduction is GOSI. Most expats see no statutory deduction at all.

Which tool should I start with?

Checking your pay? Start with the Salary or GOSI calculator. Planning a renewal or a trip? Start with the Iqama Expiry Date Planner or the Exit Re-Entry Fee Calculator.

Can I rely on the results?

Each tool applies the published rule and shows its working, so you can check it. For a binding figure, such as your final settlement or a fee at checkout, the employer's payroll or the official portal has the last word.

How often are the tools updated?

Every page shows its last-reviewed date. Pages are re-checked whenever an official rate, fee or procedure changes, such as a GOSI phase-in step or a fee update, and you can report an error through the Contact page.
