❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Saudi Utility Hub free to use?

Yes. Every calculator and guide is free, with no login, sign-up or paid tier. The site is funded by advertising through Google AdSense, which never changes how a calculator works or what a guide says.

Is the data I enter stored or shared?

No. Calculations run locally in your browser. The salary figures, dates and other values you type are not sent to our servers or stored. See the Privacy Policy for how analytics and advertising cookies work and how to turn them off.

Which calculator should I use for my end of service payment?

Use the End of Service calculator for the gratuity on its own, and the Final Settlement calculator when you also need unpaid salary, unused annual leave and deductions combined into one exit figure.

How is GOSI deducted from a Saudi salary?

For Saudi nationals, the employee share is calculated on basic salary plus housing allowance, capped at SAR 45,000 a month. Existing subscribers pay 9.75%; new entrants from August 2026 to July 2027 pay 10.75% under the phase-in set by Royal Decree M/273. Non-Saudi employees pay no employee GOSI share — the employer pays the 2% occupational hazards contribution. The GOSI calculator shows the full split.

Is there income tax on salaries in Saudi Arabia?

No. Saudi Arabia does not tax employment income, for Saudis or expatriates. The main deduction from a Saudi national's pay is the GOSI contribution; expatriates usually see no statutory deduction at all. The gross-to-net table shows common salary levels.

Which tool should I start with?

Employees checking their pay usually start with the Salary or GOSI calculator. Expats planning a renewal or a trip usually start with the Iqama Expiry Date Planner or the Exit / Re-entry planner.

Are the results official?

No. These are unofficial estimates built from the published Saudi Labour Law, GOSI rules and government fee schedules to help you prepare and sanity-check figures. Your employer's payroll, GOSI and the relevant government portal remain the authoritative sources.

How often are the tools updated?

Each page shows its own last-reviewed date. Pages are re-checked when an official rate, fee or procedure changes — for example each GOSI phase-in step or a change to the expat levy — and readers can report an error through the Contact page.
