Calculate Your Salary

Saudi Utility Hub is a small, independently operated site focused narrowly on two topics rather than trying to cover every calculator under the sun. If you're an employee trying to work out your net pay, your GOSI contribution, or what you're owed at the end of service, or an expat trying to make sense of Iqama renewal, sponsor transfer, or a family visa, the tools and guides here are built for exactly that.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Saudi Utility Hub really free to use?

Yes. Every calculator and guide on this site is completely free, with no login, signup, or paid tier. We cover running costs through advertising, which does not affect the accuracy of a calculation.

What does Saudi Utility Hub cover?

Two areas only: employment, payroll and HR tools (salary, GOSI, end-of-service, leave, working hours, and workplace-document generators), and Iqama, visa and expatriate procedures (Iqama renewal and transfer, dependent levy, family and visit visas, and related guides).

Do I need to create an account to use these tools?

No account or login is required for any calculator on Saudi Utility Hub. Every tool runs instantly in your browser.

Is the data I enter stored or shared?

No. Calculations run locally in your browser. We do not send the numbers you enter (salary, dates, etc.) to our servers or store them.

How often are the tools and guides updated?

We update tools and guides when the underlying rules change — GOSI contribution rates, Ministry of Human Resources labour regulations, Iqama fees, and immigration procedures — rather than on a fixed schedule.

Which tool should I start with?

New expats often start with the Iqama Expiry Date Estimator or a work-visa type guide. Employees checking their pay usually start with the Salary or GOSI calculator.
