Sick Leave and Maternity Leave in Saudi Arabia

How many paid sick days you get, what happens after they run out, and the longer maternity leave introduced in 2025.

Updated November 2026Saudi Labour LawFor expat employees
Home › Guides › Sick and Maternity Leave

Short answer: sick leave is paid in three tiers within a year: 30 days on full pay, 60 days on three-quarters pay, then 30 days unpaid, as long as you have an approved medical report. Maternity leave is 12 weeks on full pay since the 2025 Labour Law amendments, up from 10 weeks. New fathers get 3 days of paid leave.

Sick Leave Tiers (Article 117)

Days of sick leave in the yearPay
First 30 daysFull wage
Next 60 daysThree-quarters (75%) of the wage
Next 30 daysUnpaid

The year is counted from your first day of sick leave, not the calendar year. Example: on a SAR 6,000 wage, sick days 31 to 90 are paid at SAR 4,500 a month.

What Makes Sick Leave Valid

  • A medical report from a licensed provider, which in practice means a sick leave certificate issued through the Seha platform.
  • Tell your manager as soon as you can, and send the certificate.
  • Without an approved report, the days can be treated as unpaid absence.
Your employer can't end your contract because of illness while you are still within your sick leave entitlement.

Maternity Leave (2025 Rules)

  • 12 weeks on full pay.
  • 6 weeks must be taken after the birth. You choose how to place the rest, starting up to 4 weeks before the expected delivery date.
  • An employer can't dismiss a worker because of pregnancy or while she is on maternity leave.
  • After returning, nursing mothers are entitled to paid nursing breaks during the working day for a period after the birth.

Other Paid Leave

LeaveDays
Paternity (new father)3 days, within 7 days of the birth
Your marriage5 days
Death of a spouse, parent or child5 days
Annual leave21 days a year, 30 after 5 years with the same employer

Annual leave is separate from sick leave and isn't reduced by it. Work out your balance with the annual leave calculator.

If Your Employer Won't Pay Sick Leave

Keep the Seha certificate and your payslip showing the deduction, ask HR in writing, and if it isn't corrected, file a claim on Qiwa. See how to file a labour complaint.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many paid sick days do you get in Saudi Arabia?
30 days on full pay, then 60 days on three-quarters pay, then 30 days unpaid, within one year counted from your first sick day.
How long is maternity leave in Saudi Arabia?
12 weeks on full pay since the 2025 amendments. Six weeks must be taken after the birth.
Is there paternity leave in Saudi Arabia?
Yes, 3 days of paid leave, taken within 7 days of the birth.
Do I need a medical certificate for sick leave?
Yes. Without an approved medical report, usually issued through Seha, the days can be treated as unpaid absence.
