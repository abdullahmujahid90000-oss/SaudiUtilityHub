🏖️ Annual Leave Calculator: How Many Days Have You Earned?

21 days a year, 30 after five: see your exact balance, what it's worth in riyals if you leave, and what doesn't come out of your allowance.

📖 Articles 109 & 111 💵 Payout on leaving Updated November 2026

You've been saving up leave for a long trip home, or you've barely taken a day off in three years and you're about to resign. Either way, the same question comes up: how many days do I actually have, and what are they worth?

Short answer: you earn 21 days of paid leave a year, rising to 30 days after 5 years with the same employer. You can't cash it in while you're working there, but every unused day must be paid when you leave, at your monthly wage ÷ 30. Enter your start date below to see your balance.

    📖 How Much Leave You Earn

    Years with your employerPaid annual leave
    Under 5 years21 days a year
    5 years or more30 days a year

    Leave builds up day by day. Six months in, you've earned about 10.5 days. The jump to 30 days starts from your 5-year anniversary, and the calculator splits the period for you if you cross it.

    You can't swap leave for cash while you're still employed (Article 109). But when you leave, every unused day must be paid out (Article 111).

    📐 Real Examples

    Hassan: 3 years 5 months, hasn't taken a holiday

    About 3.41 years × 21 days ≈ 71.6 days. If he resigns now on SAR 8,000: daily rate 8,000 ÷ 30 = SAR 266.67, so about SAR 19,093 must be added to his final settlement.

    Grace: crossed 5 years in June

    Her first 5 years earn 21 days a year (105 days). From her anniversary she earns at 30 days a year, about 4.6 more days by the end of July. Total: about 109.6 days before deducting leave she has taken.

    Yousef: agreed carry-forward

    Over 7 years at 30 days a year, plus 10 days HR agreed in writing to carry forward. That written confirmation is what protects those 10 days if there's a dispute later.

    🌙 Leave That Doesn't Come Out of Your 21 or 30 Days

    TypeWhat you get
    Sick leave (Art. 117)30 days full pay, 60 days at 75%, 30 days unpaid within a year
    Maternity (Art. 151)12 weeks full pay, 6 of them after the birth
    New father3 days paid
    Your marriage5 days paid
    Death of a spouse, parent or child5 days paid
    Hajj (Art. 114)10–15 days, once, after 2 years' service

    More detail in our sick and maternity leave guide.

    Public holidays are extra too

    Eid al-Fitr (4 days), Eid al-Adha (4 days), Founding Day and National Day are paid holidays on top of your annual leave. If you work on one, those hours are overtime.

    🚫 Leave Mistakes That Cost People Money

    • "Use it or lose it." Not under the law. Unused leave doesn't simply vanish. You can agree in writing to carry some forward, and anything unused is paid when you leave.
    • Leave balance missing from the final settlement. It's one of the most common gaps. Ask HR for your balance in writing before your last day.
    • Paid at basic only. Leave payout follows your wage. Check which figure was used.
    • Forgetting the 5-year jump. Payroll systems don't always switch to 30 days automatically. Check your balance after your anniversary.

    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    How many days of annual leave do you get in Saudi Arabia?
    21 days of paid leave a year, rising to 30 days once you've completed 5 years with the same employer (Article 109).
    Is unused annual leave paid when I leave?
    Yes. Every unused day is paid at your daily wage (monthly wage ÷ 30) when your employment ends (Article 111).
    Can I sell my annual leave for cash while employed?
    No. Leave can't be exchanged for cash while you're still employed. It can only be paid out when the job ends.
    Do public holidays count as annual leave?
    No. Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Founding Day and National Day are paid holidays on top of your annual leave.
    Is sick leave taken from annual leave?
    No. Sick leave is separate: 30 days full pay, 60 days at 75%, then 30 days unpaid within a year.
    Can annual leave be carried forward?
    Yes, by agreement with your employer. Get it confirmed in writing so it's counted in your final settlement.
